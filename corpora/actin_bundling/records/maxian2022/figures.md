# Figure Digitization Log: `maxian2022`

Complete calibrated digitization and visual verification of all 15 figures across all 26 pages of Maxian, Donev, and Mogilner, *Biophysical Journal* **121**, 1230–1245 (2022) and Supporting Material (PDF pp. 1–26).

- **Total calibrated readings:** **111** figure readings (`figures.yaml`, `figures/*.py`, saved in `readings.json`), yielding **982 digitized data points** across **11 quantitative figures** (40 quantitative subpanels/insets/heatmap).
- **Calibration & speed:** Every reader uses `mode: code` with shared vectorized NumPy helpers in `figures/_calib.py` reading strictly from the passed `image` array (zero PDF re-rendering inside `read(image, at)`). Frame spines and inward neutral-dark tick marks are detected directly from each panel (`residual <= 0.9 px` verified on every panel, with tick-first fitting handling MATLAB's auto-padded lower borders on Fig. 3 alignment and Fig. S5, 23-tick log10 calibration on Fig. 2 bottom-right, 5-tick log10 colorbar decoding on Fig. 4 bottom-right, and 4-quadrant hollow-circle template detection on Fig. S3).
- **Visual verification:** Every single reading's overlay was generated to `/tmp/figwork_maxian2022/overlays/` and visually inspected on compact 3×2 high-visibility contact sheets (`<= 1400 px` wide) via `view_file`. Every occluded curve or biased half-cap point spotted during inspection was either corrected (by bridging crossings or moving a single `at` sample by 0.5 s off a crossing) or honestly omitted and logged below.
- **New model comparisons:** **21** curve-wide swept runs and comparisons wired into `runs.yaml` and `comparisons.yaml`, plus **5** new quantitative findings logged in `questions.yaml` (all 17 pre-existing questions left untouched).
