## Newly Added Open Questions and Disagreements with First Reader

- `q_eq17_detailed_balance_unit_mismatch` (`possible_error`, pp. 4–6 / 1233–1235): Recorded a dimensional mismatch not flagged by the first reader: Table 1 (p. 6) defines `k_on,s = 50 1/(um*s)` and `k_off,s = 1 1/s`, yet p. 4 omits the site-spacing factor `Delta s_u` when giving the second-end binding rate (`k_on,s`), and Eq. 17 (p. 5) equates discrete configuration probabilities `P_eq(C, x) k_on,s(ell_k') = P_eq(C', x) k_off,s(ell_k')`, which requires both transition rates in `1/s` (i.e. `k_on,s * Delta s_u`) unless `zeta(C')/zeta(C)` absorbs `Delta s_u`.
- `q_sm_c_two_fiber_sim_choices` (`choice`, pp. 20–21 / SI-3–SI-4): Recorded unstated numerical choices made by the first reader in `two_fiber_brownian_strang_sim` (`run_two_fiber_bd_small_dt`, `run_two_fiber_bd_large_dt`): SM Sec. C states `t_max = 100 s`, `N = 50`, `sigma/L = 0.005`, and `dt = 0.0005, 0.0025 s`, but omits burn-in duration (`burn_t = 1.0 s`), replicate count (`replicates = 5`), seed (`0`), and the point-spring limit (`sigma -> 0`) substituted for the finite Gaussian kernel (`sigma/L = 0.005`). Left the first reader's reconstructed model intact.
- `q_fig3_t0_and_turnover_domain_and_minor_slips` (`reading`, pp. 5, 7, 9, 12–13, 20, 23, 26): Logged five additional minor discrepancies/disagreements without altering first-reader judgements:
