- **`q_unstated_time_length_mapping` & `q_high_actin_pde_fast_regime_mismatch` — Illuminated**: Inferring `R(t) = 1/k_m(t)` from Fig. S6's left/right peak ratios yields uniform low-actin growth `alpha = 0.251` (`R(30000)/R(100) = 4.18`) and high-actin acceleration (`alpha = 0.316` over `t_tilde = 1000..10000`) followed by arrest (`alpha = 0.199` over `10000..30000`, `R(30000)/R(100) = 4.64`).
