## Summary of verification

- **Data transcription check (`data.yaml`, `source.yaml`, `references.yaml`)**: All 31 data entries, bibliographic metadata (`source.yaml`), and all 27 references (2 inline abstract references + refs 1–25 in `references.yaml`, preserving printed spellings such as `Leiken` in `ref-9`, Manning vol. `2` in `ref-11`, and duplicate `Zipursky` in `ref-12`) were checked character-by-character against `page-001.png`–`page-004.png`. Checked all 27 references against `corpora/actin_bundling/papers.yaml`; none correspond to another record in the `actin_bundling` corpus (0 missing corpus cross-links).
- **Model equation and code check (`models/*`)**: All 10 models (9 runnable + 1 non-runnable `four_sphere_helical_diffraction`) were checked against the printed formulas and word descriptions on pp. 2–4: `hexagonal_equatorial_peaks` (`q_11 = sqrt(3)*q_10`, `q_20 = 2*q_10`, p. 2), `cdw_spatial_period` (`d_CDW = 2*pi/q_z`, p. 2), `charge_patch_period` (`d = 2*d_m`, p. 4), `helical_monomer_angle` (`theta = -t*2*pi/M`, p. 3), `helical_overtwist` (`Delta_theta = (-t_1*2*pi/M_1) - (-t_0*2*pi/M_0)`, p. 3), `thermal_energy_scale` (`kBT = k_B*T`, p. 4), `twist_energy_per_monomer` (`E_twist = (1/2)*kappa*(Delta_theta)^2*d^(-1)`, p. 4), `twist_energy_kT` (`E_twist / (k_B*T)`, p. 4), `thermal_rms_twist` (`sqrt(k_B*T*d/kappa)`, p. 4), and `four_sphere_helical_diffraction` (settings `0.1` Å, `±10°`, `0.05°`, `108/51`, `[pi/4, pi/2]`, pp. 2–3). All 9 runnable models' `run()` and `symbolic()` implementations agreed to machine precision (`rel_err <= 3.3e-15`) with exact SI unit conversions (0 equation, sign, factor, or unit errors found).
- **Coverage of printed results and comparisons (`runs.yaml`, `comparisons.yaml`, `refinements.yaml`, `relations.yaml`)**: Every quantitative result the paper claims from its models is recorded and compared (15 comparisons total), including the verified major discrepancy on p. 4 (`cmp_twist_energy_kT_printed_dtheta`: `14.89 k_B T` vs printed `1.6 kT`, `not reproduced`; `cmp_twist_energy_kT_chained_dtheta`: `15.26 k_B T` vs `1.6 kT`, `not reproduced`; `cmp_thermal_rms_twist`: `0.696 deg` vs printed `≈ 3 deg`, `no verdict` due to printed `≈`, plus internal `sqrt(2)` inconsistency between `1.6 kT` and `≈ 3 deg`). No numerical-scheme or stochastic models require entries in `refinements.yaml`, and no within-record stated model relations are missing from `relations.yaml`. The 6 validator warnings on `run_twist_energy_J`, `run_twist_energy_kT_printed_dtheta`, and `run_twist_energy_kT_chained_dtheta` (mixing `90 mg/ml, 0 mM Ba2+` from `d_monomer_extracted` with `7.5 mg/ml, 60 mM Ba2+` from `Delta_theta_overtwist_printed` / `Delta_theta_36_17_calc`) accurately reflect the paper's assumption holding `d_m = 28.7 Å` fixed across the two phases.
- **Unstated inputs (`added.yaml`)**: `T_room_added` (`298.15 K`, alternatives `["293.15", "300"]`) properly captures the paper's unstated "room temperature" (p. 4, col. 1); no other numeric inputs to runnable models are unstated.
- **Confirmed first-reader judgements (no disagreement)**:
  - `b_linear_charge_spacing` (p. 1, `8634`): Recording the printed denominator spacing `≈ 2.5 Å` from `λ_A ≈ −1e/2.5 Å at pH 7` avoids writing an unprinted derived `-0.4 e/Å` and is transparently documented in its `note`.
