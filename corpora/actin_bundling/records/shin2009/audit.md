## Transcription Corrections Made

- `omega_0_printed` (p. 2, `238102-2`, col 2): corrected `printed` in `data.yaml` from `"2.8999316766"` to `"2.8999316802"`. The paper prints symbolically $\omega_0 = 12\pi/13$ rad per monomer, whose exact 10-decimal value is $2.899931680236732\dots \to 2.8999316802$; the prior entry was off by $3.64 \times 10^{-9}$ (36 ULPs in the 10th decimal place, likely from evaluating $12\pi/13$ with truncated $\pi \approx 3.14159265$). Fixing this reduces the `cmp_omega_0_from_13_6` residual from $3.64 \times 10^{-9}$ to $3.67 \times 10^{-11}$ and removes a 7th-digit drift in the 8 downstream runs using `omega_0_printed`.
