# Figure Reading Log — `liu2008`

Calibrated figure digitization of *Nature Physics* **4**, 789–793 (2008) (`corpus/actin_bundling/2008_Liu.pdf`, PDF pp. 1–16: main article pp. 1–5 / printed pp. 789–793; Supplementary Information pp. 6–16 / SI pp. 1–11). Every panel with numeric axes (9 quantitative panels, 18 calibrated series, 218 digitized points/bars in total) was read with self-calibrating `mode: code` readers (`figures/*.py`) and visually verified on high-DPI overlay PNGs in `figwork/`.

## Main Text Figures (PDF pp. 2–3 / printed pp. 790–791)

- **Fig. 1a (p. 2 / printed p. 790)** — *left out*: Phase-contrast and spinning-disc confocal fluorescence micrographs (membrane green, actin red, overlay; 5 µm scale bar) with no plotted numeric axes or data values.
- **Fig. 1b (p. 2 / printed p. 790)** — *left out*: Confocal fluorescence micrograph of AF555 actin and AF488 capping protein (10 µm scale bar) with no plotted numeric axes or data values.
- **Fig. 1c (p. 2 / printed p. 790)** — *read*: `fig1c_actin` (16 points, red curve, normalized actin line-scan, $n = 7$), `fig1c_arp23` (17 points, black curve, normalized Arp2/3 divided by normalized actin, $n = 4$), and `fig1c_cp` (20 points, blue curve, normalized capping protein divided by normalized actin, $n = 3$), all at 1500 dpi. Calibrated from the strictly uniform interior ticks (`0.2, 0.4, 0.6, 0.8` on x, residual `0.35 px`; `1.0, 1.5, 2.0` on y, residual `0.17 px`). Nature's art redraw shifted the outermost ticks inward relative to the uniform interior grid (`x = 1.0` by `32.5 px`, `y = 2.5` by `23.8 px`, `y = 0.5` by `7.8 px`); half the maximum outer-tick displacement (`~12 px`) is propagated honestly into every reading interval (logged in `q_digitize_fig1c_tick_irregularity`). Occluded curve-crossing positions (`0.20, 0.25, 0.35, 0.40` for Actin; `0.15, 0.30` for Arp2/3) are omitted so every mark sits strictly on unobstructed colored ink. Actin stays flat at `0.58–0.68 a.u.`, Arp2/3/Actin declines gently from `~0.72` to `~0.48 a.u.`, and CP/Actin rises sharply from `~0.79` (`x <= 0.55`) to `2.31 a.u.` at the protrusion tip (`x = 0.98`).
- **Fig. 1d — micrographs & inset kymograph (p. 2 / printed p. 790)** — *left out*: Time-lapse phase-contrast micrographs (5–30 min, 3 µm scale bar) and the lower-right fluorescent membrane kymograph inset (5 min, 3 µm scale bars) are microscopy images without numeric coordinate axes.
- **Fig. 1d — elongation plot (p. 2 / printed p. 790)** — *read*: `fig1d_length` (15 points along the thick black elongation polyline at `t = 0..29 min`, 1500 dpi). Calibrated from 5 unobstructed x ticks (`5, 10, 15, 20, 25 min`, residual `0.20 px`) and all 9 y ticks (`8, 10, ..., 24 µm`, residual `0.19 px`), with the inset kymograph masked out in calibrated coordinates. Digitized length rises linearly from `7.76 µm` (`0 min`) to `17.06 µm` (`9 min`) — initial slope `1.03 µm/min`, confirming `protrusion_initial_growth_rate_fig1d` (`~1 µm/min`) — then slows and plateaus at `24.13–24.63 µm` (`22–29 min`), confirming `protrusion_terminal_length_fig1d` (`~24 µm`).
- **Fig. 2a (p. 2 / printed p. 790)** — *left out*: Laser-scanning confocal FRAP image strip (Before bleach, Bleach 0 s, 280–646 s; 1 µm scale bar) with no plotted numeric axes.
- **Fig. 2b (p. 2 / printed p. 790)** — *read*: `fig2b_tip` (5 blue circles, protrusion tip), `fig2b_proximal` (5 black circles, photobleached spot edge proximal to tip), and `fig2b_distal` (5 red circles, photobleached spot edge distal to tip), all at 1500 dpi. Calibrated from all 8 x ticks (`280–630 s`, residual `0.34 px`) and all 7 y ticks (`-0.10 to +0.20 µm`, residual `0.21 px`). Circle centers are extracted by Euclidean distance transform (`>= 19.5 px` core separating markers from connecting strokes); the shared `t0 ≈ 278.4 s, ΔL ≈ 0.000 µm` origin (where the blue circle occludes the coincident black and red markers) is included for all three series with widened uncertainty on the occluded series. Across `278–645 s` (`Δt = 366 s`, matching the 5 post-bleach frames `280, 372, 463, 555, 646 s` in Fig. 2a), the tip advances monotonically to `+0.155 µm` while proximal (`-0.076 µm`) and distal (`-0.095 µm`) bleach edges show no forward elongation.
- **Fig. 3a — bundling phase diagram (p. 3 / printed p. 791)** — *read*: `fig3a_bundling_boundary` (15 points along the dark-blue phase boundary curve $D(L - L_0)$ across `40..130 nm` at $\sigma = 0.005\,k_B T/\text{nm}^2, \kappa = 20\,k_B T, L_0 = 100\,\text{nm}$, 1500 dpi). Produced by the paper's non-runnable Surface Evolver model (`membrane_bundling_surface_evolver`, `q_surface_evolver_not_runnable`).

- **Fig. s2 (p. 9 / SI p. 4)** — *read*: `figs2_sigma_0005` (16 points, blue bundling boundary at $\sigma = 0.005\,k_B T/\text{nm}^2$, `20..128 nm`) and `figs2_sigma_002` (12 points, red bundling boundary at $\sigma = 0.02\,k_B T/\text{nm}^2$, `20..68 nm`), both at 900 dpi. Calibrated from 6 top inward x ticks (`20..120 nm`, residual `3.22 px` = `0.12 nm`, slightly elevated because the `100 nm` tick overlaps the thick vertical dashed line $L_{\text{prot}}^B$) and 6 interior right inward y ticks (`20..70 nm`, residual `1.39 px` = `0.06 nm`). Vertical dashed lines sit at $L_{\text{prot}}^{F,\sigma_H} = 69.3\,\text{nm}$, $L_{\text{prot}}^B = 100.0\,\text{nm}$, and $L_{\text{prot}}^{F,\sigma_L} = 129.5\,\text{nm}$. Cross-figure check: `figs2_sigma_0005` and `fig3a_bundling_boundary` agree within uncertainty at every single one of their 14 shared $L - L_0$ positions in `[40, 128] nm` (e.g., `20.52` vs `20.55 nm` at `100 nm`; `26.80` vs `26.76 nm` at `110 nm`; max difference `1.65 nm` on the near-vertical wall at `125 nm`). Both curves stem from non-runnable `membrane_bundling_surface_evolver`.
- **Fig. s3 (p. 10 / SI p. 5)** — *read*: `figs3_osmolarity_frequency` (3 red bars with symmetric black ± s.e.m. error bars at 900 dpi: `1 = Hypoosmotic` $N = 3$, `2 = Isoosmotic` $N = 4$, `3 = Hyperosmotic` $N = 3$; `error_bars: sem`). Calibrated on y from all 6 outward ticks (`0.0, 0.2, ..., 1.0`, residual `0.95 px` = `0.0006`). Bar outer top edges align symmetrically with the midpoints of the upper and lower black error-bar caps: Hypoosmotic = `0.987` (s.e.m. `[0.970, 1.000]`), Isoosmotic = `0.908` (`[0.877, 0.939]`), Hyperosmotic = `0.752` (`[0.718, 0.783]`).
