Preserved per audit rules.
- **`q_audit_reconstructed_relations_provenance` (`rel_eq8_at_equilibrium_radius`, `rel_exact_shortening_approximated_by_eq6`, `rel_growth_ode_same_as_eq12`, `membrane_tube_equilibrium`, `compression_energy_exact_shortening`, `filopodium_growth_ode_check`; PDF pp. 11, 13 / SI pp. 6, 8):** Disagreed with tagging these three relations `provenance: stated` in `relations.yaml`, because each connects a reader-built `reconstructed` model (`R_eq = sqrt(kappa/(2*sigma))` and `f_mem,eq = 2*pi*sqrt(2*kappa*sigma)` are not printed in Liu et al., nor are the exact shortening integral or differentiated ODE) to a paper equation and is therefore a verified reader derivation (`provenance: proposed`) rather than a paper-stated relation. - **`q_audit_quarter_circle_run_negative_d` (`run_arc_quarter_circle`, `filament_wlc_arc_geometry`, `D_base_bundling_added`; PDF pp. 7–8 / SI pp. 2–3):** Noted that illustrative run `run_arc_quarter_circle` evaluates `theta = pi/2` at `L = 200 nm` (`x = 127.32 nm`) with `D = 40 nm`, yielding an unphysical negative tip separation `d = D - 2*x = -214.65 nm` (crossed filaments, since `D < 4*L/pi = 254.65 nm`). Preserved per audit rules because `run_arc_quarter_circle` feeds no comparison and `rel_arc_quarter_circle_xmax` properly maps `D = 4*L/pi`.

## Additional paper issues and unstated items added to `questions.yaml`

- **`q_audit_eq6_load_direction_wording` (`kind: possible_error`; `compression_energy_eq6`, `compression_energy_exact_shortening`, `euler_buckling_energy_eq7`, `euler_buckling_force`; PDF pp. 10–11 / SI pp. 5–6):** SI p. 5 defines `f_comp` as acting `"along the vector separating its endpoints"`, whereas SI Eq. (6) (`ΔE_comp = -(f_comp/16)*a^2*(π^2/L)`) and `f_buckle = π^2*k_B*T*L_p/(4*L^2)` use the axial shortening `Δz = π^2*a^2/(16*L)` for a vertical load along the undeformed axis; the Euclidean endpoint-to-endpoint chord of mode Eq. - **`q_audit_quarter_circle_overhang_and_Lprot_bound` (`kind: reading`; `filament_geometric_merge_bounds`, `filament_wlc_arc_geometry`, `filament_wlc_arc_by_deflection`, `L0_anchor_main`; PDF p. 8 / SI p. 3):** Supplements `q_merge_bound_finite_thickness` by noting that for a circular arc anchored normal to the base, (1) unconstrained `x(theta) = L*(1 - cos(theta))/theta` increases past `theta = pi/2` (`2/pi = 0.6366*L`) to `0.7246*L` at `theta = 2.3311 rad` (`133.56°`), so `2*L/pi` implicitly forbids downward-curving overhang (`theta <= pi/2`), and (2) requiring `L_prot = L*sin(theta)/theta - L_0 > 0` precludes a quarter circle whenever `L < (pi/2)*L_0 = 157.08 nm` (`L - L_0 < 57.08 nm`, inside the left region of Fig. 3a and Fig. s2).
