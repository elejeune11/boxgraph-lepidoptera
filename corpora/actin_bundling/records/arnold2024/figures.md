# Figure digitization inventory for `arnold2024`

Complete calibrated digitization of all quantitative plots in `corpus/actin_bundling/2024_Arnold.pdf` (PDF pp. 1–9: *Langmuir* 40, 26570–26578 (2024); PDF pp. 10–13: Supporting Information SI-1–SI-4), completed 2026-11-24. All 17 readings use `mode: code` scripts in `figures/` that calibrate axes directly from the rendered high-DPI crops and were visually verified against magenta overlay PNGs in `figwork/`.
