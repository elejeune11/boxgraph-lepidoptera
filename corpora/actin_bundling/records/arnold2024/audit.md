# Audit of `arnold2024`

Independent audit of `corpora/actin_bundling/records/arnold2024/` against `corpus/actin_bundling/2024_Arnold.pdf` (PDF pp. 1–13: main article *Langmuir* 40, 26570–26578 (2024), pp. 1–9; Supporting Information SI-1–SI-4, pp. 10–13) using all 13 page images (`pages/page-001.png`–`page-013.png`) and extracted text (`text/page-001.txt`–`page-013.txt`).

All 135 existing `data.yaml` entries were verified against the page images for digits, signs, exponents, units, and page citations (no OCR exponent, sign, or unit transcription errors were found). All 21 models (including both corrected variants for Eq. 1 and the p. 5 buckling force), 31 runs, 28 comparisons, 29 added inputs, 4 refinements, 1 stated relation, and 45 references were checked. All non-figure printed results claimed from the paper's models were already compared; quantitative plotted curves remain deferred to `digitize:arnold2024`.

## Changes made (transcription / encoding fixes and omissions)

- **`tracer_actin_mol_pct` / `actin_tracer_mole_fraction` (PDF p. 3, printed p. 26572):** Fixed a quantity misclassification in `data.yaml` where `0.5% mol G-actin-555` (labeled actin protein tracer mole percentage) was assigned to `lipid_mole_fraction` (defined in `quantities.yaml` as GUV bilayer lipid species percentage). Added dedicated quantity `actin_tracer_mole_fraction` to `quantities.yaml` and updated `tracer_actin_mol_pct.quantity`.
- **`hmm_buffer_dtt` (PDF p. 2, printed p. 26571):** Added omitted `1 mM DTT` component of the HMM freezing buffer (`"mM potassium chloride, 0.3 mM EGTA, 1 mM DTT"`) to `data.yaml`, completing the buffer record alongside `hmm_buffer_kphos`, `hmm_buffer_ph`, `hmm_buffer_kcl`, `hmm_buffer_egta`, and the DTT entries for F-, A-, and G-buffers.
- **`modified_laplace_young_eq4` (PDF p. 5, printed p. 26574):** Corrected `equation` in `models/modified_laplace_young_eq4/model.yaml` from `"P_Lo - P_Ld = lambda * div(n) + sigma_el; P_Ld^eff = sigma_el + P_Ld"` to `"P_Lo - P_Ld = lambda * div(n) + sigma_el; P_Ld^eff = sigma_el + P_out"` so that the recorded equation uses the exact symbol `P_out` printed in the text on p. 5 (leaving `model.py` unchanged since Eq. 4 itself uses `P_Ld`).
- **`passive_coarsening_scaling` (PDF p. 7, printed p. 26576):** Updated `equation` in `models/passive_coarsening_scaling/model.yaml` from `"a ~ t^(1/3)"` to `"a ≈ t^(1/3)"` to match the exact symbol printed on p. 7 (keeping `prefactor: unknown` and `model.py` unchanged).

## Disagreements with first-reader judgements (logged as `kind: reading` questions; original entries preserved)

- **`q_audit_fig_s3_mode_indices_and_L` (`fig_s3_wavevectors`, `image_width_fig_s3_fundamental_added`, `cmp_wavevector_fig_s3`; PDF p. 13 / SI-4 & p. 4 / 26573):** Disagreed with encoding unprinted Fourier mode indices `index: ["1", "2", "3", "4"]` directly inside `data.yaml` (`fig_s3_wavevectors`) and back-solving `L = 2*pi/0.9 = 6.9813 um` in `added.yaml`. Fig. S3 prints only `k = 0.9, 1.8, 2.7, 3.6 um^-1`; assuming `n = 1` makes `cmp_wavevector_fig_s3` circular at the first point (non-circularly checking only the `1:2:3:4` spacing) and yields `L = 6.98 um`, much smaller than the ~15–20 um Fig. 3A micrographs (`2.5 um` scale bar, `a` up to `3.8 um`) and inconsistent with the first reader's own `sf_test_image_width_added = 14.0 um`. Preserved the first reader's datum, added value, run, and comparison; logged as `kind: reading`.

## Additional paper inconsistencies and unstated items added to `questions.yaml`

- **`q_audit_thresholding_adaptive_vs_graythresh` (PDF p. 3 / 26572 & p. 12 / SI-3):** Logged `possible_error` for the contradiction between Methods p. 3 (`"Domains are binarized using adaptive thresholding"`) and SI Fig. S2B caption p. 12 (`"binarized using the graythresh method in MATLAB"`, which is global Otsu thresholding, not `adaptthresh`), plus unstated Grubbs test significance level $\alpha$ and MATLAB `regionprops` perimeter estimator.
- **`q_audit_fig3_duration_4000s_vs_fig3b_s3_6000s` (PDF p. 5 / 26574, p. 6 / 26575 & p. 13 / SI-4):** Logged `possible_error` for Fig. 3A caption stating a `"4000 s time-lapse"` / `"imaged for 4000 s"` for both membranes (matching Fig. 3C), whereas Fig. 3B and SI Fig. S3 plot DDM curves out to ~6000–7000 s and p. 6 reports $\tau_{\mathrm{Ld}} \approx 4100\ \mathrm{s} > 4000\ \mathrm{s}$; also noted that in Fig. S3 (2-phase left panel) the lowest wave vector $k = 0.9\ \mu\mathrm{m}^{-1}$ also does not plateau by ~6000 s.
- **`q_audit_fig4_separate_samples_incomparable_time` (PDF p. 6 / 26575, p. 7 / 26576 & p. 10 / SI-1):** Logged `reading` question capturing SI Note 1's disclosure (absent from Fig. 4 caption and p. 7 text) that Fig. 4A–B and Fig. 4C come from separate samples whose time axes `"should not be directly compared"` due to cholesterol insertion rate variability (explaining the ~5 min offset between Fig. 4C's minimum at 6 min and Fig. 4B's $\phi_{\mathrm{Lo}}$ peak at 11 min, and qualifying p. 7's attribution of `"an additional ≈25% of the membrane"` to Fig. 4C).
- **`q_audit_fig4b_error_bars_unstated` (PDF p. 6 / 26575):** Logged `unstated_input` because Fig. 4B bottom panel ($L_{\mathrm{ex}}$ vs. $t$) plots error bars without stating what they represent (SEM, SD, CI, range) or sample/domain count $n$ (unlike Fig. 4C, which states SEM and $n = 212$).
- **`q_audit_fig4c_inset_6s_vs_4s` (PDF p. 6 / 26575):** Logged `possible_error` because Fig. 4C caption states the inset shows tracer positions `"over a period of 6 s"`, whereas the inset micrograph labels only `0s`, `2s`, and `4s` (a 4 s span).
- **`q_audit_guv_lipid_pct_basis_unstated` (PDF p. 2 / 26571 & p. 4 / 26573):** Logged `unstated_input` because neither Methods (p. 2) nor Results (p. 4) specifies whether the GUV lipid percentages (`45.7% DOPC, 35% DPPC, 15% cholesterol, 4% DOTAP, 0.3% DSPE-PEG2k-FITC`) are `mol%` or `wt%` (unlike p. 3's explicit `0.5% mol G-actin-555`), nor whether `6% sucrose` (p. 2) is `w/v` or `w/w`.
