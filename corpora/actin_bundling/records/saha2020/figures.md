# Figure Digitization Log: `saha2020`

Digitized all quantitative figures of Saha, Agudo-Canalejo & Golestanian, *Phys. Rev. X* **10**, 041009 (2020) (`corpus/actin_bundling/2020_Saha.pdf`, pages 1–14) into `figures.yaml` (41 calibrated code-mode readings across 18 quantitative panels spanning all 6 figures, 399 digitized points in total), with shared image-based calibration code in `figures/_common.py` and `figures/_panels.py` and thin entry points in `figures/<id>.py`. Every single reading was verified via `boxgraph figure check` and 100% of all 41 overlay images were visually inspected via compact contact sheets in `figwork/overlay_sheets/` confirming every magenta cross sits on its intended curve. Saved readings were written to `readings.json`.
