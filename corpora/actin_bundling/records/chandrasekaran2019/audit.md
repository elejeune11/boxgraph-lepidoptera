## Disagreements with First-Reader Judgements (Left Unmodified per Audit Rules)

- **`jensen_shannon_divergence_eqS16`, `kl_divergence_gaussian_eqS17` (p. 38 / SI-19, p. 37 / SI-18):** Specializing Eqs. (S16)–(S17) to 1D Gaussian densities $P = \mathcal{N}(\mu_1, \sigma_1^2)$, $Q = \mathcal{N}(\mu_2, \sigma_2^2)$ with natural log is a model-scope choice not stated by the paper, which writes general continuous densities on p. 38 and applies them to non-Gaussian empirical distance histograms (`Dis--`, `Dis+-`, `Dis++`) in S12 Figure panel (b) (p. 37). Left untouched; logged in `q_audit_eqS16_S17_gaussian_scope_and_omml_artifacts`.
- **`rel_excluded_volume_s5_s6_printed_rescaled` (p. 32 / SI-13):** Marked `provenance: stated`, whereas SI Section 2.2.1 equates Eq. (S5) and Eq. (S6) with identical symbol $K_{\text{vol}}$ and does not state a $K_{\text{vol}} \mapsto K_{\text{vol}} L^2$ rescaling (that mapping is derived from the omitted Jacobian). Left untouched; logged in `q_audit_eqS18_S19_undefined_symbols_and_scope_notes`.
- **`nematic_order_parameter_eqS18`, `nematic_order_parameter_eqS18_sum1toN` (p. 38 / SI-19):** Parameterizing the 3D tensor Eq. (S18) via a single axisymmetric polar angle $\theta$ and returning only the prolate $\cos^2\theta \ge 1/3$ branch in `symbolic()` (while `run()` takes `max(q_xx, q_perp)`) is a reader scope choice that agrees with all tested runs. Left untouched; logged in `q_audit_eqS18_S19_undefined_symbols_and_scope_notes`.
- **`cmp_nematic_aligned_printed_N1` (p. 38 / SI-19):** The comparison note states `(not reproduced)` for printed Eq. (S18) yielding $S = 2.5$ vs. printed limit `1`, whereas BoxGraph's integer-precision band on `'1'` ($\pm 0.5$, width $1.0$) classifies $2.5$ as `close` (gap $1.0 \le$ width $1.0$). Left untouched; logged in `q_audit_eqS18_S19_undefined_symbols_and_scope_notes`.
