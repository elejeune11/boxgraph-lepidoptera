# Calibrated Figure Readings — Chandrasekaran et al. (2019)

Source PDF: `corpus/actin_bundling/2019_Chandrasekaran.pdf` (*PLoS Computational Biology* **15**(7), e1007156, 2019; DOI `10.1371/journal.pcbi.1007156`). Pages 1–19 are the main article, Methods, and References; pages 20–41 (printed SI-1 to SI-22) are the converted S1 Text Supporting Information (`s001.docx`).

All 56 calibrated figure readings (367 total points, including 222 points with SD error bars or SD bands) use `mode: code` at 900–1200 dpi with automated MATLAB plot-frame and inward-tick linear calibration (`figures/_common.py`) and fast pure-NumPy/SciPy extraction (`0.07–0.61 s` per reading directly from the cropped page array). Every reading passed `Builder.read_figure` / `boxgraph figure check` with `0 errors`, and all 56 magenta overlays were visually inspected across 10 contact sheets. Because all quantitative plots appear in the Word-converted Supporting Information as raster-embedded MATLAB graphics (`~140–300 ppi` native resolution), calibration misfits are sub-native-pixel (`0.00–3.13 px` at 900–1200 dpi). All 56 figure data are recorded with `origin: run` (outputs of the non-runnable 3D MEDYAN trajectory simulation `medyan_3d_bundle_simulation`; none of the 34 closed-form element-level models in `models/` predicts these emergent multi-filament trajectory metrics).
