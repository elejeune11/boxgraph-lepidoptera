# Figure Digitization Log: `mietke2019`

Calibrated figure digitization of Mietke, Jülicher & Sbalzarini, *PNAS* 116(1), 29–34 (2019) and Supporting Information (PDF pp. 1–23). Every quantitative plot panel across all 23 pages was digitized using `mode: code` readers (`figures/*.py` + shared `figures/_common.py`) that calibrate directly from detected neutral-dark plot borders and inward tick marks on the rendered panel crops (`1200–2400 dpi`, enforcing linear/log10 tick-fit residual `< 1.5 px` on every axis). All **26 calibrated figure readings** (**261 digitized points** across **12 quantitative panels**) were checked with `boxgraph figure check` (`figwork/<id>.png`), assembled into contact sheets (`figwork/verify_sheet_1.png`–`verify_sheet_5.png` and `figwork/verify_fig2b_top_fixed.png`), and visually inspected to confirm every magenta reading mark sits centered on its intended curve, boundary, or data marker. Saved readings are committed in `readings.json`.

## Summary of Key Verdicts Settled by Digitization

1. **Fig. 2B Hill Exponent Discrepancy (`q_fig2b_hill_exponent_discrepancy` → `q_fig2b_digitized_m1_settled`)**: Quantitatively settled — Table S1's $m = 2$ fails on all 4 curves (`close` on $\tilde{c}$, `not reproduced` on $\tilde{v}_s, \tilde{C}_s^s, \tilde{C}_\phi^\phi$), whereas $m = 1$ reproduces **52/52** calibrated points (cutting RMS by 7–22×). Meanwhile Fig. 3D and Fig. S1 / Fig. 2C confirm $m = 2$.
2. **Fig. 3A Parameter Sweep Protocol (`fig3a_xi_f0_fixed_added`)**: Holding $\xi f(c_0)/\gamma = 3$ fixed reproduces all **39/39** digitized points across all four stability and oscillatory boundaries ($\tilde{\kappa} = 0$ and $\tilde{\kappa} = 0.25$).
3. **Fig. 3D Curvature Vector-Artwork Shift (`q_fig3d_curvature_vertical_offset`)**: While $\tilde{c}$ (14/14 direct) and $\tilde{v}_s$ (14/14 with rounding) confirm the $m = 2$ tubular PDE, both bottom-right curvature curves are rigidly shifted upward by $+0.130$ (~28 px), independently proved by the periodic integral identity $\int_0^1 \tilde{C}_s^s\,d(s/L) = 0$.
4. **Fig. S1 Identity & Initial Concentration Normalization (`q_figs1_params_and_c_init_settled`)**: Fig. S1 is identified as the Fig. 2C prolate relaxation ($e = 0.75, \xi/\gamma = 15, m = 2$) initialized with conserved molecule number $c_{\text{init}}/c_0 = 4\pi R_0^2/A_0 = 0.97232$, matching $t_1 = 0$ velocities to 4 significant digits.
5. **Fig. S2A–C Convergence & Conservation (`q_figs2_convergence_prefactor_and_finite_ref`)**: Confirms $N^{-3.96}$ on $v_s$ and pole-limited $N^{-3.70}$ on $v_n$ (Fig. S2A), finite-reference steepening $1/N_t - 1/N_{t,\text{ref}}$ on Fig. S2B, and explicit-Euler conservation plateaus ($1.84\times 10^{-5}$ volume, $5.17\times 10^{-7}$ molecules) on Fig. S2C.
