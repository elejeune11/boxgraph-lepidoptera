S1–S86), table entry (Table S1), figure caption/label, parameter value, model, run, comparison, refinement, relation, and open question was checked directly against the page images (`pages/page-001.png`–`page-023.png`), page texts (`text/`), and independent symbolic/numerical derivations.

## 2. Disagreements & Numerical Observations (First Reader Choices Preserved)

- **`fig2b_steady_time_added` & `run_pde_fig2b` (`added.yaml`, `sphere_active_surface_pde`, PDF p. 4)**: The first reader's `added.yaml` entry (`printed: "8"`, `alternatives: ["5", "12"]`) was left untouched per audit rules, but its basis claim `"t/tau = 8 reaches max|v_n| < 1e-5"` is inaccurate: the cached run `run_pde_fig2b` actually outputs `pde_fig2b_vn_max_pred = 8.32e-3`. Independent time-series diagnosis shows `max|v_n|(t)` reaches a minimum of `8.2e-5` near `t/tau = 4` and then grows back up due to centered-FD Nyquist decoupling (`D1o @ D1e` in Eq. S49 with `kappa = 0`), causing BDF step-size failure before `t/tau = 10` (so the listed alternative `t/tau = 12` crashes). An independent exact steady-state (`v_n = 0`) first-order BVP solver (rms residual `5e-8`) confirms `run_pde_fig2b` at `t/tau = 8` is nonetheless accurate to 0.2% on `c(0)` (`19.919` vs exact `19.8825`) and 0.75% on `C_s^s_max` (`1.933` vs exact `1.9187`). Logged in `q_sphere_pde_nyquist_drift_fig2b`.
- **`solve_hydro_sphere` & `solve_hydro_tube` (`sphere_hydrodynamics_single_step_eqs32_33`, `tube_active_surface_pde`, PDF pp. 10, 15)**: Both hydrodynamic solvers assemble the translational-invariance Lagrange multiplier column with `Vn_lz = +inv_An * cos_psi` paired with `+sin_psi` in Eq. (S32), whereas a uniform `z`-force `f_z e_z = f_z (sin(psi) e_s - cos(psi) n)` enters Eq. (S33) with `-cos_psi`. Because `lam_z -> 0` in continuum (and `int (sin^2(psi) - cos^2(psi)) r ds > 0` for spheres, prolate spheroids, and the Fig. 2B oblate shape), this produces no error on any recorded run (`c(0)` agrees to 5 digits at `t = 4`, though `lam_z` is ~8x smaller with `-cos_psi` and `-cos_psi` guarantees a strictly positive projection `int (sin^2 + cos^2) r ds > 0` on all axisymmetric shapes). Preserved untouched per audit rules and logged in `q_sphere_pde_nyquist_drift_fig2b`.
- **`cylinder_critical_contractility` (`quantities.yaml`, `cylinder_critical_contractility_eq27`, `cylinder_critical_contractility_dimless`, PDF p. 4)**: `cylinder_critical_contractility` is declared in `quantities.yaml` but both `cylinder_critical_contractility_*` models declare their output as `dimensionless_contractility_parameter` so that `alpha_c_star_fig3_pred` can feed `run_cyl_roots_at_alpha_c`'s `alpha` port. Valid design choice by the first reader; left unchanged.

- **Printed Equations (Eqs. 1–27, SI Eqs. S1–S86)**:
  - Verified Eqs. (1)–(27) and SI Eqs. (S1)–(S86) line by line against page images and first-principles differential-geometry derivations (including covariant balance Eqs.
