# Figure Inspection — `grason2007`

Direct inspection of every page and figure panel of Grason & Bruinsma, *Phys. Rev. Lett.* **99**, 098101 (2007) (`corpus/actin_bundling/2007_Grason.pdf`, pages 1–4). All four pages were rendered at 300 dpi with pixel rulers (`figwork/p1.png`–`figwork/p4.png`) via `boxgraph figure page`, and every figure panel and inset was additionally rendered at **1200 dpi** (`figwork/fig1_1200.png`, `figwork/fig2_1200.png`, `figwork/fig2_inset_1200.png`) and visually inspected.

**Summary:** None of the figures in this paper carry calibrated numeric axis ticks, numeric data labels, or quantitative measurements/curves on a calibrated scale. Accordingly, no calibrated `figures.yaml` readings were created (`0` readings). However, 1200 dpi visual and pixel-geometry inspection of **Fig. 2 (main panel and inset)** directly confirms and corroborates two open questions in `questions.yaml` (`q_minor_typos_text_and_symbols` item 4 and `q_sigma_c_far_dimensional_and_algebraic_error`).

## Page-by-page inspection

- **Page 2 (`098101-2`, `figwork/p2.png`, `figwork/fig1_1200.png`) — Fig. 1 (top view left, side view right) — Left out:**

- **Page 3 (`098101-3`, `figwork/p3.png`, `figwork/fig2_1200.png`) — Fig. 2 (main panel) — Left out:**
  - *Visual contents:* Plot of bundle free energy $\Delta F(R)$ (vertical upward arrow with a single tick `0` at the horizontal-axis crossing, no upper/lower ticks, no numeric scale, no units) versus bundle radius $R$ (horizontal rightward arrow crossing at `0` with a single symbolic tick $\lambda_{3\perp}$ directly beneath the tangential minimum of the middle curve, no numeric ticks). Three smooth black curves originate at $(0, 0)$ and are labeled qualitatively at their right ends as $\Sigma > \Sigma_c$ (top), $\Sigma = \Sigma_c$ (middle), and $\Sigma < \Sigma_c$ (bottom), with no numeric values of $\Sigma/\Sigma_c$ stated in the figure, caption, or text.
  - *Reason left out of `figures.yaml`:* Uncalibrated qualitative diagram — no numeric tick values on either axis, completely unscaled vertical axis (`0` only), and unstated surface-tension ratios for the upper and lower curves.
  - *1200 dpi geometric check & relation to record models/questions:*
    - Pixel tracing of `figwork/fig2_1200.png` (origin `(639.5, 2291.5) px`, $\lambda_{3\perp}$ tick at `1523.5 px`, i.e. `884.0 px` per $\lambda_{3\perp}$) confirms that the three curves are exact numerical plots of Eq. (6) (`columnar_free_energy_eq6`) at $\mu = \mu_c$, $g(x; s) = -x^{4/3}/(1+x^2)^{1/3} + 2^{-1/3} s x$ ($x = R/\lambda_{3\perp}$, $s = \Sigma/\Sigma_c$), over $x \in [0, 3]$ at a single unlabelled vertical scale `2294 px` per unit of $f_0 \lambda_{3\perp}^{2/3}$:
      - Middle curve ($\Sigma = \Sigma_c$, $s = 1.00$): RMSE `1.48 px` across 28 clean columns; rises from $(0, 0)$ to a nucleation barrier at $x = 0.2432$ and touches $\Delta F = 0$ tangentially at $x = 1.0000$ ($R_0(\Sigma_c) = \lambda_{3\perp}$, matching Eq. 8).
      - Top curve ($\Sigma > \Sigma_c$): best fit $s = 1.099 \approx 1.10$ (RMSE `2.03 px`), just below the columnar spinodal threshold $s_{\text{spinodal}} = 1.1146$ ($x_{\text{inf}} = 0.5243$). It visibly retains a shallow metastable local minimum ($x = 0.6592$, $\Delta F > 0$) above the global boundary minimum at $R = 0$ ($\Delta F = 0$), matching the p. 3 col 1 discussion of metastability vs. coexistence (and mirroring the spinodal-vs.-coexistence distinction noted for the solid phase in `q_audit_solid_equilibrium_spinodal_vs_coexistence`).
      - Bottom curve ($\Sigma < \Sigma_c$): best fit $s = 0.901 \approx 0.90$ (RMSE `1.74 px`), with a negative global minimum at $x = 1.3142 > 1$ ($R_0 > \lambda_{3\perp}$).
    - *Relation to `q_f0_prefactor_factor_16_vs_256`:* Because the vertical axis has no numeric scale and $f_0$ cancels out of $R_0(\Sigma_c) = \lambda_{3\perp}$ and $\Sigma/\Sigma_c$, Fig. 2 (main panel) cannot distinguish the printed $f_0 = 3\pi(\gamma^4/(16\bar{\mu}_\perp))^{1/3}$ from the corrected $3\pi(\gamma^4/(256\bar{\mu}_\perp))^{1/3}$.

- **Page 3 (`098101-3`, `figwork/p3.png`, `figwork/fig2_inset_1200.png`) — Fig. 2 (inset) — Left out (informs open questions):**
  - *Visual contents:* Boxed schematic/uncalibrated phase diagram for the hexagonal-columnar phase in the $(\mu, \Sigma)$ plane.
    - **Horizontal axis:** Rightward arrow labeled $\mu$, carrying a single symbolic tick $\mu_c$ at its intersection with the vertical axis; no numeric ticks or scale.
    - **Vertical axis:** Upward arrow positioned at $\mu = \mu_c$, labeled $\Sigma$, carrying a single symbolic tick $\Sigma_c$ (representing $\Sigma_c(\mu_c)$; the horizontal axis represents $\Sigma = 0$, unlabeled); no numeric ticks or scale.
    - **Regions and phase boundary:** A single monotonically increasing, concave-up curve $\Sigma_c(\mu)$ runs across the $\mu < \mu_c$ half-plane from small positive $\Sigma$ at the left edge up to $(\mu_c, \Sigma_c)$. Three regions are labeled: **`bulk condensation`** throughout the right half-plane ($\mu > \mu_c$), **`dispersed filaments`** in $\mu < \mu_c$ above the curve ($\Sigma > \Sigma_c(\mu)$), and **`dispersed bundles`** (with an arrow pointing beneath the curve) in $\mu < \mu_c$, $0 < \Sigma < \Sigma_c(\mu)$.
  - *Reason left out of `figures.yaml`:* Neither axis has numeric tick values ($\mu$ has only $\mu_c$ with completely arbitrary horizontal scale; $\Sigma$ has only $\Sigma_c$ and unlabeled `0`).
  - *Impact on open questions (verified on 1200 dpi crop `figwork/fig2_inset_1200.png`, logged in `q_fig2_uncalibrated_corroboration`):*
    1. **Directly confirms `q_minor_typos_text_and_symbols` (item 4: $\mu < \mu_c$ vs. $\mu \gtrsim \mu_c$):** The inset places both dispersed phases (`dispersed filaments` and `dispersed bundles`) strictly to the left of the vertical line $\mu = \mu_c$ (i.e., $\mu < \mu_c$) and `bulk condensation` strictly to the right ($\mu > \mu_c$). This directly matches p. 3 col 1 ($\mu \lesssim \mu_c \equiv -\epsilon L/\rho_0$ and "for $\mu < \mu_c$") and confirms that the solid-phase statement on p. 3 col 2 ("near to bulk condensation ($\mu \gtrsim \mu_c$)") is a reversed-sign typo.
    2. **Directly corroborates `q_sigma_c_far_dimensional_and_algebraic_error` (exact $-1/2$ tail vs. printed $-3/2$):** Tracing the plotted phase-boundary curve ($\Sigma_c$ tick height `692.0 px` above the $\Sigma = 0$ axis; curve spanning `967.5 px` to the left of $\mu = \mu_c$) against the exact dimensionless coexistence boundary $\Sigma_c(a)/\Sigma_c(0)$ derived from Eqs. (6)–(7) (`columnar_critical_surface_tension_exact`, with $a = \pi (\rho_0/L)(\mu_c - \mu)\lambda_{3\perp}^{4/3}/f_0$) gives an exact match over $a \in [0, 10.25]$ (single fitted horizontal scale $\alpha = 0.01060\text{ px}^{-1}$) with **RMSE `0.0020`** in $\Sigma/\Sigma_c$ (`~1.4 px`, sub-linewidth). On the far-left tail ($a \in [5.3, 10.1]$), the plotted curve decays with apparent log-log slope $d\ln\Sigma_c/d\ln(\mu_c - \mu) = -0.516$ (exact theory on the same interval: `-0.4999`, already at the asymptotic $-1/2$ exponent of `critical_surface_tension_far_corrected`), ruling out the dimensionally inconsistent $(\mu_c - \mu)^{-3/2}$ expression printed on p. 3 col 2 top just above the callout to Fig. 2 (inset). This confirms graphically that Fig. 2 (inset) was generated directly from Eqs. (6)–(7) and that the printed $-3/2$ formula is an algebraic/typographical error.
    3. **Invariance to `q_f0_prefactor_factor_16_vs_256`:** Because both $\Sigma_c(\mu_c)$ and the horizontal scale of $\mu_c - \mu$ scale linearly with $f_0$ and neither axis has numeric units, Fig. 2 (inset) cannot distinguish printed vs. corrected $f_0$.
