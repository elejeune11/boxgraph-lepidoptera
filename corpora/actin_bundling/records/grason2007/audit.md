# Audit of `grason2007` (Grason & Bruinsma, PRL 99, 098101, 2007)

Independent audit of `corpora/actin_bundling/records/grason2007/` against page images `pages/page-001.png`–`pages/page-004.png` and OCR text `text/page-001.txt`–`text/page-004.txt`.

## Verification summary

- **`data.yaml`** (pp. 1, 4): All 8 data items checked against page images for digits, signs, exponents, units, pages, and quotes; no transcription errors found.
- **`models/`** (pp. 2–4): All 35 models checked against printed equations (Eqs. 5–9, inline definitions, scaling laws, and Footnote 21) and verified numerically (`run()` vs `symbolic()`); printed models faithfully implement printed equations, and all 3 `variant_of` corrections (`columnar_f0_corrected`, `critical_surface_tension_far_corrected`, `solid_harmonic_min_corrected`) are algebraically exact and sit beside the printed models.
- **`comparisons.yaml` & `runs.yaml`** (pp. 1, 4): All 3 printed model predictions (`factin_lambda_3perp_est`, `factin_cholesteric_pitch_est`, `factin_stability_threshold`) are compared (`reproduction`), and the reported F-actin bundle diameter (`factin_diameter_lai2007`) is compared (`validation`). No uncompared printed model predictions were missed (omitted qualitative numbers `2-3 filaments` and microtubule `Omega_0^-1 ~ 10 um` on p. 4 are documented scope choices in `source.yaml`).
- **`added.yaml`**: All 14 unstated inputs have clear bases, `stands_for` notes, and plausible alternatives.
- **`references.yaml`** (p. 4): All 26 references verified against `pages/page-004.png`.
- **`questions.yaml` & `relations.yaml`** (pp. 1–4): All 7 existing questions and 14 relations independently verified (including the 2D incompressible circular-disk elasticity result `3/32 * mu_perp * (Omega*R)^4` via both displacement and Airy stress function methods).

## Changes and disagreements logged

- `q_audit_solid_equilibrium_spinodal_vs_coexistence` (p. 3, `098101-3`): Added `reading` question noting that `solid_equilibrium_radius_muc` checks the spinodal bound `Sigma <= peak_val` (`0.3792 pN/nm` at benchmark F-actin parameters) rather than the thermodynamic coexistence threshold `Sigma_{c,solid}` (`0.3410 pN/nm`) where `Delta F(R_0) = 0`, so in `(0.3410, 0.3792] pN/nm` it returns a metastable local minimum with `Delta F(R_0) > 0` (unlike `columnar_equilibrium_radius_muc`, which returns `None` for `Sigma > Sigma_c`). Existing runs and relations lie well below `Sigma_{c,solid}` and are unaffected.
- `q_audit_onset_diameter_validation_prefactor_loss` (p. 4, `098101-4`): Added `reading` question disagreeing with judging `cmp_diameter_onset_from_printed_lam3_lai2007` as a hard `not reproduced` validation failure: routing the order-of-magnitude scaling estimate `factin_lambda_3perp_est` (`"~100"` nm) through `run_eq8_at_printed_lambda3` and `run_diameter_from_printed_lambda3` strips both the `~` approximation marker and the `prefactor_chained` provenance (whereas `cmp_diameter_onset_from_micro_lam3_lai2007` yields `no verdict`), treating `D = 200 nm` as an exact prefactor-1 prediction never claimed by the paper. Also notes that `run_eq8_at_printed_lambda3` pairs `lambda_3perp = 100 nm` with `f0_printed_continuum` (which corresponds to `lambda_3perp_continuum = 73.26 nm`, though `R_0` is independent of `f_0`).
- `q_audit_footnote21_stability_and_ratio_bound` (p. 4, `098101-4`): Added `possible_error` question documenting two unrecorded mathematical issues in Footnote [21] (`ref-21`, `solid_to_columnar_radius_ratio`): (1) in the harmonic solid regime, corrected Eq. (9) minimization gives stability threshold `gamma * theta_0 > 4 * Sigma` rather than the printed `gamma * theta > 2 * Sigma` (which matches either the paper's factor-of-2-inflated harmonic energy or the weaker necessary bound from dropping positive elastic terms); (2) the claim that `R_{0,solid} / R_{0,col}` is smaller "by a factor [at] least as small as `(Sigma / gamma)^(8/3)`" reverses the regime inequality, since `(Sigma / gamma)^(8/3)` is the shear-regime (`R_{0,solid} >> lambda_{3 perp}`) ratio (with exact prefactor `2^(20/3) ~= 101.6` for corrected `f_0`), whereas in the bending regime (`R_{0,solid} << lambda_{3 perp}`) `R_{0,solid} / R_{0,col}` is larger than `(Sigma / gamma)^(8/3)` (reaching `(Sigma_c / gamma)^(8/5)` at `Sigma = Sigma_{c,col}`).
- `q_audit_cholesteric_pitch_2pi_convention` (p. 2, `098101-2`): Added `reading` question noting that Eq. (3) defines `P` without a `2*pi` factor (`nabla_perp x t - z_hat / P`, i.e., `P = q_0^(-1)` rather than full cholesteric pitch `2*pi / q_0`), whereas p. 4 retains `2*pi` in `2*pi / Omega ~ 100 nm` (`factin_twist_pitch_ikawa2007`); under the full-pitch convention `q_0 = 2*pi / P_full`, the inferred pitch `factin_cholesteric_pitch_est` would be `2*pi` times larger (`~6-60 nm`). Absorbed into `prefactor: unknown` within this record, but relevant for cross-paper pitch comparisons.
