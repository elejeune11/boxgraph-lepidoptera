## Disagreements with First-Reader Judgements (Logged as `kind: reading` Questions; Original Entries Preserved)

- **`q_audit_acceleration_100mM_quantity` (`acceleration_100mM_kcl_no_profilin`, `elongation_rate_enhancement`, PDF p. 2 / p. 572):** Disagreed with assigning `quantity: fold_rate_enhancement` (`unit: percent`, `printed: "20"`) to the `+20%` acceleration at 100 mM KCl, because `fold_rate_enhancement` is defined as the total ratio `v_vasp / v_basal` (where `20%` evaluates to `0.20`-fold, an 80% reduction, rather than `1.20`-fold / `120%` of basal). Preserved per audit rules so downstream Fig. 1D digitization compares relative fractional increase `(v_vasp - v_basal)/v_basal` instead.
- **`q_audit_auc_25uM_x_bounds` (`run_auc_quintic_25uM_10krpm`, `auc_x_min_2uM_added`, `auc_x_max_2uM_added`, PDF p. 23 / Fig. S5C):** Disagreed with reusing the 2 uM actin radial window bounds (`X = 0.28–1.72 cm^2`) on diagnostic run `run_auc_quintic_25uM_10krpm`, since Fig. S5C (right panel) plots the 25 uM actin traces over `X ~ 0.20–1.54 cm^2`. Preserved per audit rules because the run is diagnostic and uncompared.

## Additional Paper Issues and Unstated Items Added to `questions.yaml`

- **`q_auc_missing_statistical_factors` (`kind: possible_error`; `auc_vasp_actin_equilibrium_quintic`, `auc_cooperative_kd_ladder`, `auc_beta_optimum_figs5b`, `auc_beta_text_rounded`, `auc_kd_four_sites_table`, PDF pp. 21–23 / Fig. S5 pp. 3–5):** Fig. S5 Eqn. 2 omits the binomial degeneracy factors `C(4, i) = (1, 4, 6, 4, 1)` for four equivalent independent binding sites on a tetramer, and pp. 22–23 erroneously treat `beta = 1` as the non-cooperative baseline. Four truly independent equivalent sites already yield macroscopic stepwise affinity ratios `3/8 = 0.375`, `4/9 = 0.444`, `3/8 = 0.375` (geometric mean `0.397`, `K_d4/K_d1 = 16`), so fitted `beta = 0.2125` (`K_d4/K_d1 = 104`) overstates physical negative cooperativity (true microscopic factor `~0.54` per step, `~6.5x` across three steps rather than `104x`).
- **`q_figs5d_miscitation_for_figs4d` (`kind: possible_error`; `bundling_fluorescence_increase_figs4d`, PDF p. 10 / p. 580):** Discussion text on p. 580 cites `(Fig. S5 D)` for parallel vs. anti-parallel filament orientation independence; Fig. S5 has only panels S5A–S5C (AUC appendix) and the intended panel is Fig. S4 D (p. S4, PDF p. 18).
- **`q_figs4d_fold_increase_definition` (`kind: reading`; `bundling_fluorescence_increase_figs4d`, PDF p. 18 / p. S4):** Fig. S4D caption defines `"fold increase per filament"` as peak intensity minus adjacent background (a difference rather than a ratio), while the bar chart plots single filaments at `~1.8–1.9` and 2-filament bundles at `~5.0–5.2` without stating the normalization/division by 2 connecting those bars to the reported `28–30%` per-filament increase.
- **`q_fig4h_unstated_fit_intercept` (`kind: unstated_input`; `k_on_actin_alone_fig4h`, `k_on_actin_plus_50nM_vasp_fig4h`, `v_max_1uM_actin_50nM_vasp`, PDF pp. 6–7 / pp. 576–577):** The Fig. 4H linear fits (`9` and `24 uM^-1 s^-1`, `"curve fit not shown"`) omit their fitted y-intercepts and critical concentrations; `24 uM^-1 s^-1 * 1 uM = 24 sub/s` is well below the measured `29.1 ± 1.2 sub/s` at 1 uM actin, implying an unstated positive y-intercept (~3–5 sub/s) or mild sub-linearity across 0.25–2 uM actin.
