# Independent Audit of `borukhov2005`

Independent second reading of `corpus/actin_bundling/2005_Borukhov.pdf` (PDF pages 1–6, printed pp. 3673–3678; PNAS 102, 3673–3678, 2005) against `corpora/actin_bundling/records/borukhov2005/`.

## Changes made (transcription, figure-reader, refinement, and arithmetic fixes)

- `fig3c_B90_instability` (`figures/fig3c_B90_instability.py`, p. 4 / printed p. 3676): Fixed a bottom-border clipping bug (`r_hi = min(int(y1) - 6, ...)` = row `1969`) that sliced off rows `1969–1971` of the 8-px native (150-dpi) curve band `1964–1971` at `c_rods = 1.0` and `1.5`, shifting `row_c` from `1967.5` (`c_linkers = 0.2189`) to `1966.0` (`0.2562`, +17% bias) and understating `line_thick` as `5 px` instead of `8 px`. Now caps `r_hi` at the exact top edge of the bottom axis border `y1_top = int(hgroups[-1][0])` (`1972`), yielding `0.2189 [0.0647, 0.3731]` at `c_rods = 1.0, 1.5` with `line_thick = 8 px` while leaving `c_rods = 1.75–2.75` unchanged.
- `fig3b_IB_onset` (`figures/fig3b_IB_onset.py`, p. 4 / printed p. 3676): Fixed a 1-px bleed into row `1964` of Panel b's bottom axis border (`1964–1979`) at `c_rods = 1.5` caused by `r_hi = int(round(my * 0.12 + by))` = `1965`. Capping `r_hi` at `y1_top = int(hgroups[-1][0])` (`1964`) restores the exact 8-px native curve band `1956–1963` (`row_c = 1959.5`, `c_linkers = 0.2595 [0.0948, 0.4242]` instead of `0.2470 [0.0699, 0.4242]`).
- `ref_cone_cubatic_ngamma` (`refinements.yaml`, p. 4 / printed p. 3676): Changed watched `output` from `c_C_right` to `c_C_left` on `run_cone_cubatic_3c`. In `models/cone_cubatic_coexistence/model.py`, `c_C_right` (the `C/(N+C)` boundary) never calls `w_I` and had identically zero dependence on `n_gamma` (`changes = [0.0, 0.0]`); only `c_C_left` (the `(I+C)/C` boundary) uses the `n_gamma` quadrature grid (converging cleanly at $O(n_\gamma^{-2})$, `changes ~ 1e-8`).
- `q_eq8_parallel_sign` (`questions.yaml`), `second_virial_eq8_corrected` (`models/second_virial_eq8_corrected/model.yaml`), and `borukhov2005` (`source.yaml`, p. 3 / printed p. 3675): Corrected an arithmetic error in the first reader's prose that stated printed Eq. 8 diverges to `-2.35e63` at `u_par = 0` (`X = -157.5`). Direct evaluation gives bracket `1 + (exp(-X) - 1)/X = -1.60e66` and `B(0 deg) = -2.32e64` (`-2.3250e64`, 10× larger in magnitude than `-2.35e63`).
- `effective_potential_eq1_eq3_corrected` (`models/effective_potential_eq1_eq3_corrected/model.yaml`, p. 2 / printed p. 3674): Completed the `equation:` metadata string to include `; Gamma(gamma) = (L/D)*Gamma_par (gamma <= D/L) else Gamma_perp/sin(gamma)`, matching `model.py` and the parent model `effective_potential_eq1_eq3`.
- `L_printed` (`data.yaml`, p. 4 / printed p. 3676): Updated `citation.quote` from `"the size of the binding protein"` (which belonged to the following $\delta$ clause) to `"length is L ⫽ 1,000 nm; the size"` verbatim from `text/page-004.txt`, directly quoting the printed rod length `L = 1,000 nm`.
- `ref-30` (`references.yaml`, p. 6 / printed p. 3678): Restored printed German umlauts in `Hüttelmaier` and `Schlüter` to match p. 6 verbatim.

## Disagreements and additional open questions added (first-reader judgements preserved)

- `q_reading_intermediate_criterion_and_rounding` (`questions.yaml`, p. 4–5 / printed pp. 3676–3677; about `eps_perp_3b_printed`, `intermediate_eps_perp_criterion`, `intermediate_eps_perp_criterion_corrected`, `cmp_intermediate_eps_perp_printed`, `cmp_intermediate_eps_perp_corrected`): Disagreed with treating `eps_perp = -10.5` as `origin: predicted` from exact $B(0^\circ) = B(90^\circ) = 0$ (the paper states that criterion only qualitatively as "approximately the same linker chemical potential" on p. 4 col. 1, whereas `-10.5` is a chosen parameter in the full-free-energy intermediate window) and flagged that BoxGraph's `input_rounding` varies the exact chosen integer inputs (`D=8, delta=1, kappa^-1=1, n_perp=1, eps_1=-7, eps_par=-7`) by `±0.5`, widening the band to `[-12.70, -6.56]` and causing both `-9.63` (corrected) and `-9.65` (printed, wrong crossing direction) to be misclassified as `reproduced` despite a `0.87 k_B T` discrepancy.
- `q_reading_cubatic_cone_validity` (`questions.yaml`, p. 4 / printed p. 3676; about `cone_cubatic_coexistence`, `cmp_fig3b_C_right`, `cmp_fig3c_C_left`, `cmp_fig3c_C_right`): Noted that in the reconstructed `cone_cubatic_coexistence` model, `w_N_small(alpha)` omits `-exp(-Gamma_perp/sin(gamma) + |u_perp|)` from Eq. 8, yet along `run_cone_cubatic_3c`'s `C/(N+C)` boundary (`c_l = 20–44`) the optimal cubatic cone half-angle reaches `alpha_C = 0.7854 rad` (hitting the `pi/4` bound at `c_l = 20`) to `0.5720 rad` (at `c_l = 44`), where `exp(-Gamma_perp/alpha_C + |u_perp|) = 3.79–2.53` is order unity rather than negligible.
- `q_eq6_B_definition_dimensions_and_u_overload` (`questions.yaml`, pp. 2–3 / printed pp. 3674–3675; about `effective_potential_eq1_eq3`, `second_virial_eq8`): Recorded two additional printed notation/dimension inconsistencies not previously logged: (1) below Eq. 6 (p. 3, col. 2), $B(\gamma_{12}) = \int dr_{12}[1 - \exp(-u(r_{12}, \gamma_{12}))]$ omits the normalization $1/[(\pi/4) L^2 D_{\text{eff}}]$ required to match dimensionless Eq. 8; (2) $u(\gamma)$ is overloaded between Eqs. 1–2 (attraction alone) and Fig. 2 / p. 3 col. 1 (net well-bottom potential $\Gamma(\gamma) - |u_{\text{attr}}(\gamma)|$).
- `q_reading_fig3b_left_boundary_omitted` (`questions.yaml`, p. 4 / printed p. 3676; about `fig3b_C_right_data`, `cone_cubatic_coexistence`): Noted that Fig. 3b's solid left boundary of the cubatic phase (`(I+C)/C` and `(C+B)/C`) was not digitized in `figures.yaml` (nor noted as omitted in `figures.md`) despite `run_cone_cubatic_3b` already producing `cone_cubatic_3b_left_pred`, and noted the 1-native-pixel floor (`~0.20`) at `c_rods = 1.0–1.5` in `fig3c_B90_instability`.
