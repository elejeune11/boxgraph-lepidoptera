# Calibrated Figure Readings — Chandrasekaran et al. (2024)

Source PDF: `corpus/actin_bundling/2024_Chandrasekaran.pdf` (*Nature Communications* **15**, 3139, 2024; DOI `10.1038/s41467-024-46726-6`). Pages 1–14 are the main article and Methods; pages 15–36 are the Supplementary Information (`MOESM1_ESM.pdf`); page 37 is the Supplementary Movie descriptions (`MOESM4_ESM.pdf`).

All 106 calibrated readings use `mode: code` at 900–1200 dpi with automated plot-frame and tick-centroid linear calibration (`figures/_plot_common.py`). Every reading passed `boxgraph figure check` and visual overlay inspection (`figwork/<reading_id>.png`). Main-article vector panels achieve sub-half-pixel calibration misfit (`0.00–0.42 px` at 1200 dpi); raster-embedded Supplementary Information panels achieve sub-native-pixel misfit (`0.00–3.75 px` at 900–1200 dpi).
