## 2. Disagreements & Audit Reading Notes (First-Reader Judgements Preserved)

- **`B0_seed_norm`, `kinetic_pde_cylinder_conserved_cartesian_mem`, `kinetic_pde_cone_conserved_cartesian_mem`, `q_membrane_laplacian_comsol`, `q_vca_4fold_time_window` (pp. 7, 18, 20, 24 / SI 7, SI 9, SI 13):** Logged in `q_audit_reading_seed_norm_and_40nM_peak`. With the first reader's peak-amplitude-`1 /um^2` Gaussian seed (`exp(-r^2/(2*sigma^2))`), the 1D-Cartesian-membrane cylinder variant produces an early (`t <= 20 s`) peak of only `406.2 /um^2` at `40 nM CP` (lower than `B(40 s) = 417.1 /um^2` at `mesh_n = 24`, which drops `-2.3%` to `407.7 /um^2` at `mesh_n = 48`), and sits ~8–12% below Fig. 5c (`407, 258, 182` vs `~440, ~285, ~200 /um^2`) and Fig. 5f (`351, 225, 145` vs `~400, ~250, ~150 /um^2`). Independently testing a 1D-normalized Gaussian seed `(1/(sqrt(2*pi)*sigma))*exp(-r^2/(2*sigma^2))` (peak `39.89 /um^2`) on the same Cartesian-membrane PDE matches Fig. 5c (`B_peak = 448.5, 288.2, 203.2`; `B_40 = 416.0, 172.8, 97.8`) and Fig. 5f (`B_peak = 393.4, 255.1, 164.6`) closely while giving slightly lower `A*_peak` (`73.5–79.0` vs `~83 /um^2`) and ~8–11% higher Supp Fig. 14 `V_tot` (`2170, 2068, 1667`). Also noted that prose inside the preserved first-reader questions `q_membrane_laplacian_comsol` and `q_vca_4fold_time_window` quotes pre-refinement numbers (`371, 233, 145`; `2072, 1924, 1513`; `4.0–4.1x` at `100–200 s`), whereas the current cone PDE gives `351.3, 225.5, 145.2`, `1978, 1877, 1513`, and VCA enrichment `2.3–2.7x` (`t = 40 s`), `3.5–3.8x` (`t = 100 s`), and `4.4–5.4x` (`t = 200 s`, crossing `~4x` near `110–140 s`, where finite-pool CP depletion also induces a secondary barbed-end surge to `535–759 /um^2`).
- **`cone_disk_radius`, `r_cone_40nM`, `r_cone_80nM` (p. 22 / SI 11):** Logged in `q_audit_reading_cone_radius_tangent_geometry`. While the first reader's equal-area/equal-volume formula `r_cone = 2*R/sqrt(N)` gives `9.00 um` (`N = 16`, `close` to `10 um`) and `6.09 um` (`N = 35`, `reproduced` vs `6 um`), a tangent circular cone of height `R = 18 um` subtending solid angle `4*pi/N` (`r_cone = R * tan(arccos(1 - 2/N))`) gives `9.96 um` (`N = 16`, `10.14 um` for protrusion median `15.5`) and `6.36 um` (`N = 35`), matching both printed radii to 1 significant figure. Preserved the first reader's model.
- **`vesicle_increase_cp_dox`, `cp_beta2_overexpr_fold`, `expression_fold_change` (p. 7 / 7):** Logged in `q_audit_reading_quantity_and_180nM_cortex`. `vesicle_increase_cp_dox` (`~50%` relative increase) and `cp_beta2_overexpr_fold` (`2.1`-fold ratio) share the quantity `expression_fold_change`, though fold-ratio and relative percentage change are distinct dimensionless physical quantities. Preserved without changing existing ids/quantities.
- **`frac_cortex_120nM` (p. 3 / 3, p. 13 / SI 2):** Logged in `q_audit_reading_quantity_and_180nM_cortex`. Main text p. 3 groups `120 and 180 nM` CP together as `"60%"` closed cortex formation, whereas Supp Fig. 4 (p. 13) shows `~60%` at `120 nM` (`~28%` flat + `~32%` concave) and `~68%` (all flat) at `180 nM`.
- **Minor non-quantitative typos noted in paper (outside quantitative scope):** p. 3 Fig. 1e caption omits `"profilin"` after `"13.5 µM"`; p. 9 Methods prints `"2 h at −4 °C"` for Arp2/3 dye incubation (cf. `"2 h at 4 °C"` for VCA ten lines below); p. 25 SI Methods prints `"should me added"`.
