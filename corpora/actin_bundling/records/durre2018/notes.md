## 1. Pair 1: `durre2018` ↔ `liu2008` (`ref-21`, Membrane-Tethered Arp2/3 Dendritic Networks on GUVs)

- **Citation & `cited.yaml` (`0` links)**: `durre2018` cites `liu2008` (`ref-21`, `source: liu2008`) strictly qualitatively in two Introduction sentences on p. 2 (external GUV actin networks generating inward protrusions by membrane self-stabilization, and in vitro reconstitution precedent). All `8` `from_source` datums in `durre2018/data.yaml` cite external primary literature (`supp-ref-3` Marchand 2001, `supp-ref-5` Pollard et al. 2000, `supp-ref-6` Schafer et al. 1996, `supp-ref-7` Lanni & Ware 1984). Both bibliographies share `6` foundational references (`Mullins 1998`, `Loisel 1999`, `Bernheim-Groswasser 2002`, `Spudich & Watt 1971`, `Egile 1999`, `Wiesner 2003`).
- **ModelAtlas (`0` relations) & Validations (`0` entries)**: Across all `19 × 24` model pairs, the two studies formalize strictly complementary halves of GUV Arp2/3 membrane deformation: `durre2018` solves 2D/3D reaction-diffusion PDEs (`V, V*, A*, V+, B, G, Arp, CP`) explicitly neglecting membrane mechanics (SI p. 10), while `liu2008` solves Helfrich/WLC filament-membrane mechanics (bundling, Euler buckling, tube force `f_mem = 2π√(2κσ)`) and a fixed-`N` single-tube Brownian-ratchet ODE (`Eq. 12`) with empirical monomer decay `c_0(t) = 1 - I(t)/I(∞)`. None of `durre2018`'s runnable outputs predicts any of `liu2008`'s `18` `origin: measured` datums.
- **Sharp quantitative contrasts (logged in `q_liu2008_cp_switch_ratchet_and_no_cross_validation`)**:
  1. **Leaflet topology & CP switching**: `liu2008` assembles externally in an infinite bath (`8.5 μM` G-actin, `160 nM` Arp2/3, `400 nM` His-N-WASP, `0 nM` CP, no profilin), producing thin parallel-filament inward membrane tubes (`1–25 μm`, `N ≈ 10`) in `~95%` of GUVs (`> 10 μm`), and reports that adding CP during assembly inhibits protrusions (p. 1). `durre2018` encapsulates internally in a finite lumen (`3 μM` G-actin, `300 nM` Arp2/3, `300 nM` His-VCA, `13.5 μM` profilin): `0 nM` CP gives `96%` bulk networks (`4%` cortex, `0%` protrusions); `20 nM` CP localizes `78%` to the membrane (`41.5%` cortex, `36%` domains); `40 nM` CP peaks broad convex outward protrusions (`24.3%` of GUVs, `≤ 4 μm`); `60–180 nM` CP switches to concave inward domains (`38.0%` at `60 nM`) and re-entrant closed cortices (`59.9%` at `120 nM`, `67.7%` flat at `180 nM`).
  2. **Extrapolating `durre2018/kinetic_ss_infinite_pool_conserved` to `liu2008`'s `CP = 0` assay**: At `G_0 = 8.5 μM, Arp_0 = 0.16 μM, CP_0 = 0 nM, V_0 = 1084 μm⁻²`, the conserved 1D steady state yields `B_ss = 10,576 μm⁻²` (`97.0%` boundary-layer G-actin depletion, `G_mem = 0.256 μM`, branching rate `10.58 μm⁻² s⁻¹`). Tripling Arp2/3 (`0.48 μM`, which cut filopodia `~60%` in `liu2008` via branch-limited `L_prot^B`) raises `B_ss` `2.32×` to `24,528 μm⁻²` (branching `24.53 μm⁻² s⁻¹`), while lowering Arp2/3 to `10 nM` (no drop in `liu2008`) still sustains `B_ss = 1,366 μm⁻²`. Under `durre2018` standard inputs, exact steady-state extinction (`B_ss → 0`) occurs at `CP_extinct = 485.0 nM` (`305.3 nM` under `liu2008` concentrations).
  3. **Audit of `durre2018` Discussion p. 8**: Two qualitative claims on p. 8 are quantitatively challenged: (a) "protrusion velocity increases exponentially with an increasing number of barbed ends" misstates the Brownian ratchet (`liu2008` Eq. 12), where `exp(-f_mem δ / (N k_B T))` grows sub-exponentially and saturates toward `1` (`0.269` at `N = 10`, `0.769` at `N = 50`, `0.877` at `N = 100`, `0.971` at `N = 450` for `f_mem = 20 pN`, `δ = 2.7 nm`, `k_B T = 4.114 pN·nm`, only `+11%` across `100 → 450`); and (b) "protrusion velocities are not dominated by monomer depletion" conflicts with `durre2018`'s own conserved 1D steady state, where membrane `G_mem_ss` is already depleted by **`85.1%`**, **`70.5%`**, **`48.8%`**, and **`35.3%`** at `20, 40, 80, 120 nM` CP (`0.448, 0.884, 1.535, 1.942 μM` vs `3.0 μM` bulk).

## 2. Pair 2: `durre2018` ↔ `gov2018` (Membrane-Actin Coupling, Activator Clustering & Curvature Modes)

- **Citation & `cited.yaml` (`0` links)**: Contemporaneous 2018 articles; neither cites the other, though `durre2018` cites `Gov & Gopinathan 2006` (`ref-1` = `gov2018/ref-34`) and `Orly, Naoz & Gov 2014` (`ref-6`) qualitatively on pp. 2 and 8.
- **ModelAtlas (`0` relations) & Validations (`0` entries)**: Disjoint frameworks (`19 × 37` pairs audited; `gov2018` contains `0` measured datums). Specifically, `durre2018/stokes_einstein_scaling` (`D = D_ref (M_ref/M)^(1/3)`) is intentionally NOT linked to `gov2018/cmc_diffusion_einstein` (`D = Λ k_B T`): `Λ k_B T` is a 1-op (`too_plain`) Einstein identity without mass `M`, and 2D membrane proteins follow logarithmic Saffman–Delbrück drag rather than 3D globular `M^(-1/3)` scaling.
- **Sharp quantitative contrasts (logged in `q_gov2018_curvature_sorting_vs_kinetic_enrichment_and_concave_mode`)**:
  1. **Curvature sorting (`gov2018` Eq. 2.9) cannot explain `durre2018`'s `~4×` VCA clustering**: Although `durre2018` p. 8 cites `ref-1` (Gov & Gopinathan 2006) as theoretical agreement for VCA up-concentration, `gov2018/cmc_steady_state_enrichment_eq2_9` (`n/n_0 = exp[(κ/k_B T)(a/R)]`) at `durre2018`'s measured domain curvatures `|H| = 0.07, 0.10, 0.13, 0.22, 0.50 μm⁻¹` (`R = 2–14.3 μm`) with `κ/k_B T = 20` predicts only **`1.007×–1.051×`** (`a = 5 nm`) or **`1.014×–1.105×`** (`a = 10 nm`) — and even at `gov2018`'s extreme upper bound `κ/k_B T = 100, a = 10 nm`, only **`1.139×`** at `120 nM` median (`|H| = 0.13 μm⁻¹`) and **`1.649×`** at `|H| = 0.50 μm⁻¹` (`χ = 0.007–0.50` vs `ln 4 = 1.386`). Consistent with VCA staying homogeneous without actin (Fig. 2e–f), `durre2018`'s cone PDE generates `~4×` enrichment purely kinetically on a flat rigid membrane via paused-state immobilization (`V+`, `k_re = 0.001 s⁻¹`).
  2. **Concave bending without myosin or concave CMCs**: In `gov2018` Sections 2–4, protrusive actin coupled to concave CMCs (`H_0 > 0, A > 0`) is linearly stable; static inward invaginations require contractile force (`A < 0`). `durre2018`'s myosin-free concave domains (`-0.07` to `-0.50 μm⁻¹`, `38.0%` at `60 nM` CP) arise instead from spatially non-uniform barbed-end density (`B_rim > B_center` at `80 nM` CP) where faster rim growth bends the patch inward; adding `5 μM` NMM II at `120 nM` CP doubles curvature (`-0.13 → -0.22 μm⁻¹`) and induces fission in `27.5%` of vesicles, bridging into `gov2018`'s contractile instability.
  3. **2D membrane mobility**: Mapping `durre2018`'s FRAP `D_V = 1.59 μm²/s` through `gov2018/cmc_diffusion_einstein` gives `Λ_V = D_V / k_B T = 386.5 μm/(pN·s)` (`k_B T = 0.004114 pN·μm`).

## 3. Pair 3: `durre2018` ↔ `graham2023` (Confined GUV Actin Cortices/Patches vs VASP Droplet Shells/Rings)

- **Sharp quantitative contrasts (logged in `q_graham2023_confinement_shell_ring_vs_cp_cortex_and_no_validation`)**:
  1. **Confinement-driven vs CP-gated peripheral shells**: `graham2023` confines CP-free, Arp2/3-free actin (`~12.6×` partitioned inside `D = 1–4 μm` VASP droplets, `R = 0.5–2 μm ≪ L_p = 17.7 μm`), where filament bending rigidity forces peripheral shells that condense into equatorial rings (`T_ring ~ D^1.72–1.97`) and stretch droplets outward into rods (`AR > 9–15`). `durre2018`'s `10×` larger GUVs (`D = 20–50 μm`, `R = 10–25 μm ≳ L_p`) form `96%` bulk networks (`4.0%` cortex) at `0 nM` CP; peripheral cortex assembly requires membrane-tethered VCA + CP and is re-entrant (`41.5%` at `20 nM` → `10.2%` at `40 nM` → `1.0%` at `60 nM` → `59.9%` at `120 nM` → `67.7%` flat cortex at `180 nM`).

## 4. Pair 4: `durre2018` ↔ `nastkolb2022` (Same Bausch Lab: GUV Arp2/3+VCA vs Planar SLB/ML VASP Bundles)

- **Citation, ModelAtlas & Validations**: `0` cited links (`nastkolb2022` cites `durre2018` qualitatively on pp. 2 and 9 for profilin+CP bulk suppression and activator clustering/phase separation; `nastkolb2022/data.yaml` has `0` `from_source` entries; `1` shared protocol ref `Spudich & Watt 1971`), `0` ModelAtlas relations (`19 × 17` pairs audited), `0` cross-validations (`66` measured Arp2/3-free VASP SLB/ML datums audited).
- **Sharp quantitative contrasts (logged in `q_nastkolb2022_same_lab_cp_ratio_frap_and_enrichment_audit`)**:
  1. **Identical membrane recipe & CP:actin suppression window, divergent architectures**: Both Bausch-lab studies use `87.4–87.45%` Egg-PC / `10%` Ni-NTA / `2.5%` PEG2000-PE membranes. `nastkolb2022` adopts `50 nM` CP + `10 μM` profilin + `2 μM` actin (`R_CP = 0.025`, profilin:actin `5.0:1`), sitting directly inside `durre2018`'s `60–80 nM` CP concave-domain window (`R_CP = 0.020–0.0267`, profilin:actin `4.5:1`), yet His-VASP resists CP to grow `1–3 μm`-wide apolar bundles (`45 ± 12` filaments/μm) instead of concave dendritic patches. Furthermore, `durre2018` Supp Fig. 7 uses a planar lipid monolayer for TIRF domain-growth kinetics, whereas `nastkolb2022` proves lipid monolayers slow Ni-NTA-bound VASP mobility **`4×`** (`t_1/2 = 0.399` vs `0.102 min`, mobile fraction `0.61` vs `0.97`) and suppress bundling.
  2. **Empirical test of `durre2018/stokes_einstein_scaling` on 2D Ni-NTA membranes**: Converting `nastkolb2022`'s circular-spot FRAP (`w = 2.48 μm`, `D ≈ 0.224 w²/t_1/2`) yields `D_VASP,SLB ≈ 0.225 μm²/s` (`Λ ≈ 54.7 μm/(pN·s)`, **`7.1×`** slower than `durre2018`'s monomeric His-VCA `D_V = 1.59 μm²/s`) and `D_VASP,ML ≈ 0.058 μm²/s` (`27.6×` slower). Scaling `D_V = 1.59 μm²/s` from `12 kDa` His-VCA to `~160 kDa` tetrameric His6-VASP via `durre2018/stokes_einstein_scaling` predicts `0.671 μm²/s` (**`2.98×` too fast**), demonstrating that multivalent Ni-NTA membrane anchoring (`4` lipid tethers) and glass SLB drag outweigh 3D globular `M^(-1/3)` scaling.
  3. **Direct FRAP verification of polymerization-induced nucleator immobilization**: `durre2018` SI p. 13 postulates an immobile paused state `V+` ("immobilization due to filament binding or reduced diffusivity due to molecular crowding") to explain `~4×` VCA enrichment; `nastkolb2022` directly measures this phenomenon for His-VASP on the identical bilayer — SLB mobile fraction drops from **`0.97` pre-polymerization to `0.65` after `30 min`**, while VASP peaks at **`1.95×`** enrichment on growing bundles at `5.1 min` (`fig5c_vasp`, relaxing to `1.62×` at `60.8 min`) alongside a transient **`~9%`** depletion of unbound tracer lipids (`0.910` at `4.8 min`, `fig5c_lipids`).
