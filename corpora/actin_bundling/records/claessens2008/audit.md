# Audit of `claessens2008`

Independent second reading of Claessens et al. (2008), *PNAS* 105(26):8819–8822 (PDF pp. 1–4) and Supporting Information (PDF pp. 5–7), verified against `pages/page-001.png`–`page-007.png` and `text/page-001.txt`–`page-007.txt`.

All 61 data entries in `data.yaml` (digits, signs, exponents, units, and pages) and all 7 unstated inputs in `added.yaml` (bases and real physical/methodological alternatives; no non-physical perturbations of exact identities) were verified against the page images.

## Corrections made

- `diameter_scaling_Rstar` (p. 1 / 8819, p. 2 / 8820): corrected `equation` in `model.yaml` from `"D ~ (R*)^0.3"` to `"D ≈ (R*)^0.3"` to match the approximation symbol literally printed on pp. 1–2 (keeping `prefactor: unknown` unchanged).
- `diameter_filament_scaling` (p. 5 / SI 1): corrected `equation` in `model.yaml` from `"D ~ n_f^(1/2)"` to `"D ≈ n_f^(1/2)"` to match the approximation symbol literally printed on p. 5 (keeping `prefactor: unknown` unchanged).
- `binding_energy_fascin` (p. 3 / 8821): corrected `equation` in `model.yaml` from `"DeltaG / (k_B * T) = ln(K_D / c_0)"` to `"Delta G = k_B T ln(K_D)"` so the recorded equation matches the paper literally as printed (keeping `c_0` as an added input port and `model.py` unchanged).
- `helical_overtwist_from_symmetry` (p. 2 / 8820): fixed `model.py` `run()` to compute `Delta_theta = th0 - th1` (`360 * (s_1/p_1 - s_0/p_0)`) instead of `abs(th1 - th0)` so `run()` agrees in sign with `model.yaml`'s declared `equation` and `symbolic()` for all inputs (leaving the `-13/6 -> -28/13` value `+0.9890 deg` unchanged).

## Printed results, runs, and comparisons added

- `cmp_Rsat_stoich_vs_Rstar_plateau` (p. 1 / 8819, p. 2 / 8820, p. 5 / SI 1): added `consistency` comparison of `Rsat_stoich_pred` (`1/4.5 = 0.2222`) against `Rstar_plateau` (`≈ 0.25`) to check the paper's claim that the bundle-diameter plateau corresponds to fascin binding saturation.
- `run_Etwist_fascin_half_sat_printed_dtheta` and `cmp_Etwist_fascin_half_sat_printed_dtheta` (p. 2 / 8820): added symmetric `1/2`-variant run and `reproduction` comparison (`4.67 k_B T` vs `E_twist_per_fascin_printed` `≈ 10 k_B T`) matching the printed model's `Delta_theta_max` + `Rsat_stoich_pred` run.
- `run_Etwist_fascin_Rstar_0p5` and `cmp_Etwist_fascin_Rstar_0p5` (p. 2 / 8820, p. 3 / 8821): added run and `consistency` comparison evaluating `twist_energy_per_fascin` at the printed high-$R^*$ SAXS condition `Rstar_fig3b` (`0.5`, `-28/13` overtwist), giving `4.06 k_B T` vs `E_twist_per_fascin_printed` (`≈ 10 k_B T`), directly testing the paper's claim that $E_{\text{twist}}$ is constant per bound fascin across all concentrations.
- `run_Etwist_fascin_half_Rstar_0p5` and `cmp_Etwist_fascin_half_Rstar_0p5` (p. 2 / 8820, p. 3 / 8821): added matching `1/2`-variant run and `consistency` comparison at `Rstar_fig3b` (`0.5`), giving `2.03 k_B T` vs `E_twist_per_fascin_printed` (`≈ 10 k_B T`).

## Disagreements and newly logged open questions

- `q_twist_radian_unit_and_high_Rstar` (p. 2 / 8820, p. 3 / 8821): logged `reading` question noting (1) the unstated degree-to-radian conversion for $\Delta\theta$ in $E_{\text{twist}} = \tau(\Delta\theta)^2 d^{-1}$, (2) the unstated per-fascin formula and high-$R^*$ drop to `4.06 k_B T` (`2.03 k_B T` with `1/2`) at printed `R* = 0.5`, and (3) mixed evidence on treating `twist_energy_per_monomer_half` / `twist_energy_per_fascin_half` as a misprint correction since the printed formula without `1/2` reproduces `≈ 10 k_B T` at saturation (`9.14–9.34 k_B T`) while the `1/2` variant gives `4.57–4.67 k_B T`.
- `q_scaling_approx_provenance` (p. 1 / 8819, p. 2 / 8820, p. 5 / SI 1): logged `reading` question documenting that $D \approx (R^*)^{0.3}$ and $D \approx n_f^{1/2}$ are printed with `≈` and omit dimensional prefactors in nm, and that SI p. 5 does not state the plateau diameter $D_{\max}$ used to convert Fig. 1C's $D(R^*)$ into Fig. S1's $R^*(n_f)$.
- `q_SI_hex_derivation_and_R_quantity_boundary` (p. 5 / SI 1, p. 6 / SI 2): logged `reading` question documenting (1) the unstated exact hexagonal-shell bond-counting derivation $R = \frac{1}{u}(3 \pm 3\sqrt{\frac{4}{3} - \frac{1}{3n_f}}\,n_f^{-1/2})$ and the small-$n_f$ artifacts of rounding to $3.5\,n_f^{-1/2}$ (negative inside-only values for $n_f < 1.361$; `6.5/13 = 0.500` instead of `6/13 = 0.462` at $n_f = 1$), (2) the Fig. S1 caption wording slip ("over bound actin monomer"), and (3) a reading disagreement that `bundle_geometry_saturation` declares output `molar_ratio_abp_actin` (`R`) whereas Fig. S1 plots those curves on the `R*` (`effective_bound_ratio`) axis and compares them to `Rstar_plateau` (`≈ 0.25`), blocking direct comparison unless bridged.
- `q_fig3a_inset_12_21_degeneracy` (p. 2 / 8820, p. 3 / 8821): logged `possible_error` question noting that Fig. 3A Inset marks `(2,1)` and `(1,2)` as two separate peaks at distinct $q$ values between `c` (`1.46 nm^-1`) and `(3,0)` (`1.755 nm^-1`), even though `(2,1)` and `(1,2)` are strictly degenerate at $\sqrt{7}\,q_{10} = 1.548\text{ nm}^{-1}$ on a 2D hexagonal lattice and no hexagonal Bragg reflection exists between $\sqrt{7}\,q_{10}$ and $3\,q_{10}$.
- `q_reading_model_scope_and_conventions` (p. 2 / 8820, p. 3 / 8821, p. 4 / 8822, p. 5 / SI 1, p. 7 / SI 3): logged `reading` question recording three disagreements retained without altering first-reader scope: (1) `droplet_ring_filament_number` and `droplet_critical_concentration` are marked `from_words: true` rather than `reconstructed` despite unstated formula/molar mass/ring circumference (and exact ~2.0x offset vs Fig. 2E's $n_f = 20$ line); (2) `helical_symmetry_ratio` outputs positive $M = p/s$ vs printed `-13/6` and `-28/13`, and Fig. S2 Right orange-boxes monomers `{1, 6, 7, 10, 11, 15}` spanning `n_unit = 15` (vs `n_unit_28_13_added = 14`), where `n_unit = 15` with $\phi_0 = 0^\circ$ gives a strictly smaller max mismatch (`8.57 deg`, total `25.71 deg`) than the paper's `10.7 deg` (`38.6 deg`); and (3) `binding_energy_fascin` (`described_from: ref-36`), `DeltaG_fascin_printed` (`from_source: ref-36`), and `Kd_fascin` (`conditions: {temperature: "20 degC"}`) attribute the textbook equation and `20 degC` condition to ref-36 even though ref-36 supplies $K_d = 0.3\,\mu\text{M}$ and `20 degC` is this paper's polymerization temperature.
