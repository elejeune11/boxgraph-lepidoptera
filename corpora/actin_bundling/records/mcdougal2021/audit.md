# Audit of `mcdougal2021`

Independent audit of `corpora/actin_bundling/records/mcdougal2021/` against all 25 page images (`pages/page-001.png`–`pages/page-025.png`) and text files (`text/page-001.txt`–`text/page-025.txt`) of `corpus/actin_bundling/2021_McDougal.pdf` (PNAS 118(49), e2112009118, pp. 1–8; SI Appendix, pp. 9–25). Figures were not digitized in this audit.

## Verification summary

- **`data.yaml` (80 entries)**: Every entry checked against its page image for digits, sign, exponent, unit, printed page label, and quote. No digit, sign, exponent, unit, or page transcription errors were found.
- **Models (19 models, `model.yaml`, `model.py`, `symbolic()`)**: Every printed equation (Eqs. 1–4 on p. 7 and SI p. 11; `freq = s^-1` on SI pp. 23–24) and verbal processing formula (`I = A^2`, bounding-box `V` & `S`, plane-extrapolation `L = z_tip / sin(theta)`, trough-connecting baseline peak height, pooled mean & SD, pupal `%` development and inverse, axial step count, plus reconstructed stand-ins for double-pass phase-to-height and Rayleigh resolution) verified against the page images; all `model.py` and `symbolic()` implementations match their declared equations and units.
- **`added.yaml` (24 entries)**: All 24 unstated inputs verified to be used in `runs.yaml`, with clear `basis`, `stands_for`, and two realistic `alternatives`.

## Changes made

- **`cmp_fig5_implied_duration_40`** (p. 5, `comparisons.yaml`): Added consistency comparison between the already-computed `fig5_implied_T_pupal_40` (from `run_fig5_implied_duration_40pct`: 114 h at roughly 40% -> 11.88 d) and `pupal_duration_typical` (`"about 10"` d, p. 2), completing the symmetrical pair alongside `cmp_fig5_implied_duration_35` (from 100 h at roughly 35% -> 11.90 d).
- **`q_audit_reference_arm_typo`** (pp. 6, 10; `questions.yaml`, `kind: possible_error`): Added open question for the textual error repeated in both main text Materials and Methods (p. 6) and SI Detailed Methods (p. 10, line 61), which state `"We place the pupa under the reference arm"` instead of the sample arm (contradicting the preceding microscope description, the gold-coated reference coverslip mirror paragraph, Fig. 2F p. 3, and SI Fig. S1 p. 12).
- **`q_audit_curvature_vs_radius`** (p. 11; `questions.yaml`, `kind: possible_error`): Added open question for the dimensional/geometric slip in SI Detailed Methods (p. 11, lines 109–110), which defines the scale width measurement region as `"where the curvature of the scale’s edge exceeds the scale length"` (curvature has dimension `1/[length]` and is small on a nearly straight edge; evidently **radius of curvature** exceeding scale length was meant).

## Disagreements with first reader (logged as `kind: reading` questions; first-reader entries preserved)

- **`q_audit_figS2_colorbar_ticks`** (p. 13; about `figS2CD_phase_grad_axis_max`, `figS2F_phase_grad_mag_max`, `cmp_figS2_grad_mag_bound`): Disagreed with treating the highest printed colorbar tick labels `40` (SI Fig. S2C–D) and `60` (SI Fig. S2F) as exact upper bounds in `cmp_figS2_grad_mag_bound`, which yields a `not reproduced` verdict (`sqrt(40^2 + 40^2) = 56.57` vs `60 rad/um`). On `page-013.png`, the colorbars in C–D visibly extend past `±40` (just as B extends past `±3` to `±pi`) and F extends slightly past `60`, so `not reproduced` reflects evaluating the norm on rounded axis ticks rather than a discrepancy in the paper.
- **`q_audit_fft_dc_and_findpeaks`** (pp. 11, 23; about `dominant_spatial_frequency_fft_1d`, `profile_slope_rotation_and_peaks`, `figS10_dominant_freq`, `figS10_ridge_spacing`): Disagreed with two `from_words: true` implementation choices that will affect downstream verification once SI Fig. S10B–D are digitized: (1) `dominant_spatial_frequency_fft_1d/model.py` subtracts `mean(y)` before the 1D FFT, whereas SI Fig. S10D (`page-023.png`) clearly retains a large nonzero DC peak (`~4.4 x 10^3 nm` at `f = 0`, indicating an uncentered, unnormalized FFT whose DC sinc sidelobes can shift low-frequency peaks); (2) `profile_slope_rotation_and_peaks/model.py` uses `scipy.signal.find_peaks` with `prominence` (`min_peak_prominence_nm = 15.0`) rather than MATLAB `MinPeakHeight` (`"minimum accepted peak height and spacing"`, SI p. 11), and both models embed reader-chosen default settings (`n_fft = 4096`, `f_min = 0.3`, `n_uniform = 256`, `min_peak_prominence_nm = 15.0`, `min_peak_distance_um = 0.8`).
- **`q_audit_figS8_tilde_range`** (p. 21; about `smooth_epicuticle_phase_window`, `irregular_surface_phase_window`): Disagreed with transcribing `(34~36% of development)` and `(37~39% of development)` on `page-021.png` as `"~34 to 36"` and `"~37 to 39"`. There is no leading `~` printed before `34` or `37`; the medial `~` functions as a wave-dash range separator (`34 to 36`, `37 to 39`), contrasting with the prefix `~` in `"From ~39% onward"` in the next sentence (though the shaded region boundaries in Fig. S8D on `page-020.png` lie near `~36.5%` and `~39.5%`, making an approximate reading plausible as well).
