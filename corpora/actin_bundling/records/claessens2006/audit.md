Audited `corpora/actin_bundling/records/claessens2006/` against all 20 PDF page images (`pages/page-001.png`–`pages/page-020.png`) and extracted text (`text/page-001.txt`–`text/page-020.txt`) of `corpus/actin_bundling/2006_Claessens.pdf` (Claessens, Bathe, Frey & Bausch, *Nature Materials* 5, 748–753, 2006 + Supplementary Information NM06040751A).

## Changes Made

- `q_fig3a_caption_symbol_colors_swapped` (pp. 16, 19; `questions.yaml`, `source.yaml`): Added `possible_error` question documenting that the Fig. 3a caption on p. 16 prints a red open circle (`○`) for 1:50 and a blue filled circle (`●`) for 1:2, whereas Fig. 3A on p. 19 plots blue open circles (`○`) and red filled circles (`●`) (consistent with the red-filled = high / blue-open = low convention in Fig. 3B–D). Updated `source.yaml` notes to flag this caption color swap for `digitize:claessens2006`.

- `q_sampling_window_vs_relaxation_time` (pp. 11, 20; `questions.yaml`): Added `reading` question flagging a disagreement on `t_sampling_window` (see Disagreements below).
- `q_unstated_kd_and_delta_max_sources` (p. 12; `questions.yaml`): Added `unstated_input` question noting that $K_d = 0.5\ \mu\text{M}$ (α-actinin, plastin), $K_d = 0.7\ \mu\text{M}$ (fascin), $\delta_{\max} = 33\text{ nm}$, and the claim that droplet $c_{\text{ABP}}$ is independent of finite-size effects appear on p. 12 without any literature citation or experimental assay description.
- `q_si_fig1a_stiffness_vs_fig3b` (pp. 19, 20; `questions.yaml`): Added `unexplained_difference` question flagging that `run_ring_stiffness_si1a` gives $\kappa_B \approx 52.7\ \text{pN}\cdot\mu\text{m}^2$ at $R_{\text{ring}} = 8.0\ \mu\text{m}$, $D \sim 0.08\ \mu\text{m}$ (SI Fig. 1a, fascin 1:2), roughly 2–2.5× stiffer than all fascin 1:2 data points in Fig. 3B (p. 19), and correspondingly produces a $6.9\times$ theoretical relaxation-time ratio (`run_relaxation_si1b` / `run_relaxation_si1a`) versus $< 2\times$ apparent saturation-time ratio in SI Fig. 1a–b.
- `source.yaml` (`notes`, pp. 8, 20): Corrected two factual slips in prose notes: (1) clarified brush-border microvilli $\alpha \le 0.00284 \ll 1$ at $\delta = 100\text{ nm}$ (the value computed by `run_alpha_microvilli_hi`) and $\le 0.0086 \ll 1$ at saturated $\delta = 33\text{ nm}$ (previously only `0.0086` was written); (2) corrected SI Fig. 1a red point count from `8 red points` to `10 red points` (`page-020.png` shows 10 points in SI Fig. 1a at 1, 3, 5, 10, 15, 20, 30, 40, 50, 60 × 117 ms frames, and 8 points in SI Fig. 1b).

## Disagreements with First Reader (Preserved per Audit Rules)

- `t_sampling_window` (p. 11; `data.yaml`, `quantities.yaml`): The first reader assigned `t_sampling_window` (`5 to 12 s`, fluorescence observation/averaging duration) to `quantity: relaxation_time`, which is defined as the WLC bundle equilibration relaxation time $\tau \sim (\mu/\kappa)L^4$ (SI p. 20). These are distinct physical quantities of dimension `[time]`. Preserved without modifying `data.yaml` or `quantities.yaml`, and logged as `q_sampling_window_vs_relaxation_time` (`kind: reading`).
- `run_ring_stiffness_si1a`, `run_ring_stiffness_si1b` (pp. 5, 11; `runs.yaml`, `added.yaml`): The first reader supplied `T: T_room_p5` (`298 K`, `from_source: ref-23` on p. 5) for Claessens et al.'s own ring measurements instead of declaring an added ambient temperature in `added.yaml` (e.g., 293–298 K). Preserved the first reader's runs and logged the unstated experimental temperature in `q_unstated_measurement_temperature`.
- `run_bundle_2d_fascin_05_n2d3` (pp. 12, 20; `runs.yaml`): Uses `n: n_2d_eval_3_added` ($n_{2\text{D}} = 3 \Rightarrow n_{3\text{D}} = 9$) together with `L_B: L_B_fascin_05_n10` (evaluated at $n = 10$, $30.2\ \mu\text{m}$, rather than $n = 9$, $28.8\ \mu\text{m}$). Preserved as an exploratory evaluation run choice (not compared against any printed datum).
- `kappa_B_fig3a_plastin_05_n36_eye`–`kappa_B_fig3d_peg4_n36_eye` and `D_sat_si1a_eye`–`tau_eq_si1b_eye` (pp. 16, 19, 20; `data.yaml`): All approximate eye-read anchor values (`~`) were visually verified against `page-019.png` and `page-020.png` as reasonable handoff placeholders (`no verdict`) for `digitize:claessens2006`; preserved as entered.
