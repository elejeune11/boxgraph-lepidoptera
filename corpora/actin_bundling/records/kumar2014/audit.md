## Disagreements (logged without altering first-reader judgements)

- `t_max_2d_added`, `run_pde_2d_fig3b`, `advection_reaction_diffusion_pde_2d` (PDF p. 4 / `208101-4`, Fig. 3): Kept `t_max_2d_added = 120` and `stochastic: false` unchanged per audit rules; logged in `q_audit_2d_pde_horizon_and_stochasticity` that `run_pde_2d_fig3b` actually outputs `state_code = 2.0` (`osc_amp = 0.1325 > 0.1`), contradicting `t_max_2d_added`'s basis (`"120 tau reaches settled stationary hexagons at (beta = 1.3, P = 6.2)"`), while extending to `t_max = 240` settles `seed = 42` cleanly to `state_code = 1.0` (`osc_amp = 0.0035` at `n_grid = 64`, `0.0014` at `n_grid = 128`; `A_max = 4.093 -> 4.207`, a 2.78% settled grid shift), and varying `seed` in `{42, 43, 44}` at `t_max = 120` flips `state_code` between `2.0` and `1.0` and shifts `A_max` by 3.1%.
- `run_ard_stab_fig3_hopf` (PDF p. 4 / `208101-4`, Fig. 3): Kept run wiring unchanged per audit rules; logged in `q_audit_ard_hopf_beta_mismatch` that mixing `beta = 1.9` onset inputs with `A0_fA_beta3` / `I0_fI_beta3` (`beta = 3.0`) preserves `tr_tau_L = 0` but outputs `det_ard_hopf = 1.4358, im_ard_hopf = 1.1982` (`beta = 3.0`) instead of `det_tau_L = 0.5242, im_lam_plus = 0.7240` (`beta = 1.9`).
- `cmp_peclet_physical_vs_fig1e` (PDF pp. 2–3 / `208101-2`–`208101-3`): Kept comparison unchanged per audit rules; logged in `q_audit_peclet_tautological_comparison` that `zdm0_added = 1.2 Pa` was tuned to `P_fig1e = 1.2`, making `cmp_peclet_physical_vs_fig1e` circular.
- Minor scope/terminology choices left unchanged:
  - `active_stress_regulation_eq11` (PDF p. 2, Eq. 11) parameterizes Eq. (11) with a single saturation ratio `xi = A_s/A_0 = I_s/I_0` (as specialized on p. 3) rather than independent `A_s/A_0` and `I_s/I_0`.
  - `source.yaml` `notes` refers to the PDE time-stepper in `advection_diffusion_pde_1d` and `advection_reaction_diffusion_pde_2d` as `"ETD2RK"`, whereas the implemented scheme is an integrating-factor RK2 (Heun/trapezoidal) corrector with an ETD1 predictor (still 2nd-order accurate in `dt`, verified by `ref_pde_1d_fig2_dt`, `ref_pde_1d_fig1e_beta3_dt`, and `ref_pde_2d_fig3b_dt`).
  - `cmp_v_homog_steady` (PDF p. 2) carries `conditions_note = "condition 'spatial_dimension' named by one side only"` because `run_v_homog` passes `nu_1d_printed` (tagged `spatial_dimension: "1"`) whereas `v_homog_steady` (`v = 0`) holds in arbitrary dimension `d`.
