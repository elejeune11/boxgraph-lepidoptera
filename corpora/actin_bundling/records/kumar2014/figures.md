# Figure Readings — `kumar2014`

Calibrated `mode: code` digitization of all quantitative panels in K. Vijay Kumar, Justin S. Bois, Frank Jülicher, and Stephan W. Grill, *Pulsatory Patterns in Active Fluids*, Phys. Rev. Lett. **112**, 208101 (2014) + Supplemental Material (`corpus/actin_bundling/2014_Kumar.pdf`, PDF pages 1–6).
