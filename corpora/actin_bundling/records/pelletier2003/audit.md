## New open questions added

- `q_audit_gamma_dimer_monomer_and_pellet_stoichiometry` (`unstated_input`, p. 1, `148102-1`): Logs two unstated stoichiometry inputs affecting all reported molar ratios `gamma` (`gamma_phase_separation_threshold`, `gamma_fig1a`, `gamma_fig2`, `gamma_saxs_onset`, `gamma_peaks_threshold`) and `actin_conc_saxs_final`: (i) whether `gamma` counts α-actinin dimers (~200 kDa) or monomers (~100 kDa) per G-actin monomer (a factor-of-2 ambiguity), and (ii) that SAXS measures phase-separated centrifuged pellets rather than bulk mixtures, leaving the actual actin concentration and bound α-actinin:actin ratio inside the measured pellet unstated.
- `q_audit_saxs_resolution_and_two_point_q2_scaling` (`unstated_input`, p. 3, `148102-3`): Logs the unstated SSRL beamline 4-2/10-2 instrumental `q`-resolution `Delta_q_inst` (whether `dq1_saxs = 0.056` and `dq2_saxs = 0.136 nm^-1` are raw or deconvolved, noting quadrature deconvolution would steepen the width ratio further and widen the first-peak Scherrer domain-size discrepancy) and notes that only two diffraction peaks (`q_1 = 0.19`, `q_2 = 0.29 nm^-1`) are observed, giving an exact two-point empirical exponent `alpha = 2.10` (`1.87`–`2.37` under printed rounding) that cannot by itself discriminate `q^2` paracrystalline broadening from alternative broadening laws.
- `q_audit_mesh_size_persistence_length_and_mgcl2_kcl` (`unstated_input`, p. 2, `148102-2`): Logs for `network_mesh_size_fig1a`, `mgcl2_conc_fig1b`, and `kcl_lscm` that (i) neither the measurement method/uncertainty for the `~5 um` mesh size nor a numerical persistence length is stated (despite 1:1 Alexa-Fluor 488 Phalloidin stabilization increasing F-actin persistence length to ~15–18 um vs ~9–10 um bare), and (ii) it is unstated whether the `200 mM KCl` LSCM ionic-strength adjustment was omitted or retained in the `80 mM MgCl2` counterion control in Fig. 1(b), where monovalent KCl shifts divalent-counterion bundling thresholds (`ref-13`, `tang1996`).
