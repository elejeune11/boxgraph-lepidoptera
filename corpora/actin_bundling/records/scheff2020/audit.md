# Independent Audit of `scheff2020`

Audited source: Danielle R. Scheff, Kimberly L. Weirich, Kinjal Dasbiswas, Avinash Patel, Suriyanarayanan Vaikuntanathan, and Margaret L. Gardel, *"Tuning shape and internal structure of protein droplets via biopolymer filaments"*, *Soft Matter* **16**, 5659–5668 (2020), DOI `10.1039/c9sm02462j` (`corpus/actin_bundling/2020_Scheff.pdf`, PDF pages 1–10 Advance Article proof + PDF page 11 ESI Fig. S1 and Movie S1–S2 captions).

## Summary of Independent Verification

- **Page images and text (pp. 1–11)**: All 11 page images (`pages/page-001.png`–`page-011.png`) and OCR text files (`text/page-001.txt`–`page-011.txt`) were independently read line by line. All OCR misreads (`μ` -> `m`, `~` -> `B`, `<` -> `o`, `>` -> `4`, `°` -> `1`) were verified against the rendered page images.
- **Metadata & references (`source.yaml`, `quantities.yaml`, `references.yaml`)**: Bibliographic metadata, all 46 quantity dimensions, and all 50 citations on pp. 9–10 (including corpus links `ref-19 -> weirich2017` and `ref-42 -> weirich2019`, and verbatim preservation of printed citations such as duplicated refs 4/36 and 24/47) were checked against the page images with zero transcription errors.
- **Data (`data.yaml`, 58 datums)**: Every digit, sign, inequality/approximation marker, unit, page number, quote, and condition across all 58 datums — including actin/FUS/KCl/capping-protein concentrations (pp. 2, 4–6), monomer rise `2.7 nm` (p. 2), filament lengths `~270 / ~135 / ~90 nm` (p. 6), filament diameter `~8 nm` (p. 8), partitioning ratios (p. 4), droplet counts `n = 1180, 1308, 410` (pp. 6–7), all 9 Fig. 4(a) shape percentages (`73/24/3`, `11/83/6`, `0/32/68`, p. 7), and all 18 fitted `K/gamma` and `omega` values from Fig. 5(c) (p. 8) and Fig. S1 left/right (p. 11) — matched the PDF page images verbatim. All 58 citations pass `quote_on_page`.
- **Added inputs (`added.yaml`, 25 entries)**: Every unstated input (`cp_conc_mid_added`, `cp_mol_pct_mid_added`, probe areas/volumes/axes/angles/material scales/coalescence inputs) is separated from `data.yaml` and carries an explicit `basis`, `stands_for`, and at least two plausible `alternatives`.
- **Printed equations and independent symbolic/numerical derivations (pp. 2–8)**:
  - *Bispherical coordinate metric (p. 3)*: Symbolically verified from `rho = sin(xi)*sin(eta)/Z`, `z = cos(xi)/Z`, `Z = 1 + sin(xi)*cos(eta)` that the coordinate system is orthogonal (`g_{xi,eta} = 0`) with exact scale factors `h_phi = sin(xi)*sin(eta)/Z`, `h_xi = 1/Z`, `h_eta = sin(xi)/Z`, boundary `eta_0 = 2*atan(1/x)`, and circular-arc meridians `(rho + cot(eta))^2 + z^2 = csc^2(eta)`.
  - *Eqs. (2A) and (2D) (p. 3)*: Independently checked closed forms against adaptive numerical quadrature (`scipy.integrate.quad`/`dblquad`) to `< 1e-14`, confirming spherical limits (`f_s(1) = 4*pi`, `v(1) = 4*pi/3`) and cylindrical asymptotics (`f_s(x) = 8*pi/(3*x) + 8*pi/(5*x^3) - 8*pi/(21*x^5) + ...`, `v(x) = 16*pi/(15*x^2) + 32*pi/(105*x^4) - 16*pi/(315*x^6) + ...`).
  - *Eq. (2B) bend misprint & Eq. (2C) anchoring (p. 3)*: Independently constructed the axisymmetric quasi-bipolar unit director tangent to circular arcs meeting at `(0, +-y)` in cylindrical coordinates `(rho, z)` and evaluated `(div n)^2`, `|curl n|^2`, and `(n . N_hat)^2` symbolically and numerically:
    - Exact `(div n)^2 = 4*cos^2(xi)/N` (matches Eq. 2B).
    - Exact `|curl n|^2 = sin^2(xi)*sin^2(eta)/N`, confirming that printed Eq. (2B)'s `sin^2(xi)*cos^2(eta)` is a genuine misprint (nonvanishing on the straight central axis `eta = 0` and yielding `0.1341*pi` vs Williams 1985 exact `0.5326*pi` for the ideal bipolar sphere `x = 1, y = 1`, ratio `0.2517`).
    - Exact `(n . N_hat)^2 = (y^2 - 1)^2 * cos^2(xi) * sin^2(eta_0) / (4*N)` (matches Eq. 2C verbatim).
    - Confirmed that `quasi_bipolar_elastic_factor_printed` and downstream `*_printed` models implement the printed `cos^2(eta)` verbatim, while `variant_of` models (`*_exact_bend`, `*_frank_half`) isolate the evidenced corrections.
  - *Omitted Frank `1/2` prefactor in Eq. (1) + Eq. (2B) (p. 3)*: Independently confirmed via ideal bipolar sphere (`x = 1, y = 1`): Eq. (2B) integrates `phi` over `[0, 2*pi]` without `1/2`, giving splay factor `8*pi` (`F_splay = 8*pi*K*R`) instead of standard Frank `4*pi*K*R` (Williams 1985 / Prinsen & van der Schoot 2004). Verified that `*_frank_half` variants apply the `1/2` factor once (no double-halving).
  - *Quasi-bipolar quadrature, algebraic substitution, and optimization*: Verified `t = 1/y^2` scaling (`t2N = t^2 * N`), `xi -> pi - xi` symmetry, quadratic clustering Jacobian `dxi = pi*s*ds`, cross-model agreement between `quasi_bipolar_free_energy_*` and individual shape-factor models (`diff <= 8.88e-16`), and minimizer convergence against finer quadrature (`n_xi = 96, n_eta = 48, xtol = 1e-9`; `dx <= 4e-7`, `dF <= 1e-13`).
  - *Fig. 5(c) & Fig. S1 volume-scaling discrepancy (pp. 3–4, 8, 11)*: Independently verified that under the printed scaling `c = K/(gamma*V^(1/3))`, equilibrium aspect ratio decays asymptotically as `x_eq - 1 ~ A^(-1/2)` and predicts at `A = 20 um^2`:
    - `L ~ 135 nm` (`K/gamma = 1.7 um`, `omega = 0.45`): `x_eq = 1.461` (printed), `1.485` (exact bend), `1.454` (exact bend + Frank 1/2), `1.415` (printed bend + Frank 1/2) under `A_spindle`; across all 4 area conventions (`A_spindle`, `pi*R*r`, `V^(2/3)`, `R*r`) and all 4 model variants, `x_eq` lies in `[1.363, 1.487]` (far above Fig. 5c's plotted red curve).
    - `L ~ 270 nm` (`K/gamma = 1.7 um`, `omega = 2.8`): `x_eq = 2.629` (printed), `2.633` (exact bend), `2.262` (exact bend + Frank 1/2), `2.220` (printed bend + Frank 1/2) under `A_spindle`; across all 16 model/area combinations, `x_eq` lies in `[1.903, 2.667]` (far above Fig. S1 right's plotted red curve).
    - Independently verified that replacing `c = V_tilde^(-1/3)` with `V_tilde^(-1) = (K/gamma)^3 / V` (missing cube root) and setting `A = R*r` with exact bend (no `1/2`) yields steep `A^(-3/2)` decay (`L ~ 90 nm`: `1.219 -> 1.027`; `L ~ 135 nm`: `1.492 -> 1.076`; `L ~ 270 nm`: `2.871 -> 1.085` across `A = 2` to `35 um^2`), tracking the curves in Fig. 5(c) and Fig. S1.
  - *Refinements (`refinements.yaml`, 14 checks) and relations (`relations.yaml`, 9 checks)*: All 14 numerical refinements settle (`shift <= 1e-7`, well inside `0.01%–0.05%` tolerances) and all 9 within-paper relations pass `boxgraph relations --only scheff2020` (`verified`).

## Changes Made During Audit

- **`quasi_bipolar_surface_factor_closed` (`models/quasi_bipolar_surface_factor_closed/model.py`, p. 3 Eq. 2A)**: Fixed an algebraic bug in the high-aspect-ratio (`x > 1e3`) Taylor expansion of `f_s(x)`: changed the `O(x^-5)` coefficient from `-8/105` to the exact `-8/21` (`f_s(x) = pi*u*(8/3 + 8/5*u^2 - 8/21*u^4 + ...)`, reducing relative error against `symbolic()` at `x = 1000.0001` from `1.14e-13` to machine epsilon `2.07e-16`).
- **`tactoid_geometry_from_semimajor` (`models/tactoid_geometry_from_semimajor/model.py`, p. 3 Eq. 2A & circular-arc spindle geometry)**: Fixed two algebraic bugs in the `x > 1e3` asymptotic branches: (i) `_fs(x)` `O(x^-5)` coefficient `-8/105 -> -8/21`; (ii) `_a_spindle(x)` `O(x^-3)` coefficient `+16/15 -> +8/15` (and added `-8/105 * inv^5`), resolving an `8.89e-08` mismatch between `run()` and `symbolic()` at `x = 1500` (now `1.63e-16`).
- **`quasi_bipolar_aspect_vs_area_printed`, `quasi_bipolar_aspect_vs_area_exact_bend`, `quasi_bipolar_aspect_vs_area_frank_half` (`models/quasi_bipolar_aspect_vs_area_*/model.py`, p. 3–4)**: Fixed the same `_a_spindle(x)` `x > 1e3` asymptotic branch bug (`+16/15 * inv^3 -> +8/15 * inv^3 - 8/105 * inv^5`) for consistency with `tactoid_geometry_from_semimajor`.
- **`run_area_exact_bend_270nm_small`, `run_area_frank_half_270nm_small` (`runs.yaml`, pp. 3, 4, 11) and `cmp_135nm_large_exact_bend_vs_upper_bound`, `cmp_135nm_large_frank_half_vs_upper_bound`, `cmp_270nm_small_exact_bend_vs_upper_bound`, `cmp_270nm_small_frank_half_vs_upper_bound` (`comparisons.yaml`, pp. 3, 8, 11)**: Added missing `variant_of` runs and comparisons against `observed_aspect_ratio_upper` (`< 2.5`) so that both corrected variants (`exact_bend` and `frank_half`) are compared against every printed datum that `quasi_bipolar_aspect_vs_area_printed` is compared against (confirming all three variants exceed `2.5` at `A = 5 um^2` for `L ~ 270 nm`: `2.941`, `2.928`, and `2.633`).
- **`q_270nm_batch_variation_fig3c_vs_figS1` (`questions.yaml`, pp. 2, 3, 6, 11)**: Added a new `unexplained_difference` open question documenting the previously unrecorded discrepancy between the two nominally identical `1 uM actin, L ~ 270 nm, 4.4 uM FUS` populations in Fig. 3(c)/(d) (p. 6, `n = 1180`, area up to `120 um^2`, aspect ratio `< 2.5`) versus Fig. 3(g) and Fig. S1 right (pp. 6, 11, area up to `40 um^2`, aspect ratio reaching `3–4`, `n` unstated), linked to the same-day batch protocol on p. 2 col 2.
