## 3. Disagreements with First Reader (Logged as `reading` Question; First-Reader Entries Preserved)

- **`q_audit_reading_140mM_salt_origin` (`c_salt_coac`, `S_salt_screened_added`, `S_salt_zero_added`, `cmp_lT_screened_vs_04nm`; p. 21 / SI-10):** SI Section I.h.5 states `≈ 140 mM salt` without citing or deriving it, whereas every experiment in the paper uses `50 mM KCl, 1 mM MgCl2, 1 mM EGTA, 10 mM imidazole (pH 7.0), 72–150 µM ATP` plus `5 mM` counterions from `pLK·HCl`/`pRE·Na` (~55–65 mM monovalent salt equivalent; ionic strength `I ~ 65–70 mM`; charge-squared free-ion sum `sum_gamma Z_gamma^2 * c_gamma = 2*I ≈ 135–140 mM`). `added.yaml` interprets `140 mM` as monovalent salt molarity (`2 * 0.14 = 0.28 mol/L`, giving `S_salt_screened_added = 2.28 mol/L` when combined with `2 mol/L` covalently linked opposite-chain residues). If `140 mM` instead already denotes `sum_gamma Z_gamma^2 * c_gamma = 0.14 mol/L`, Eq. (9) gives `l_T = 4.499 Å = 0.450 nm` (`S_salt = 2.14 mol/L`, on the rounding boundary of `0.4 nm`) or `5.059 Å = 0.506 nm` (`S_salt = 0.14 mol/L`, salt-only). Preserved the first reader's `added.yaml` entries and logged this disagreement in `questions.yaml`.
