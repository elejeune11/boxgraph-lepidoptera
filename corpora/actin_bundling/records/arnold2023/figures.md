# Figure digitization inventory for `arnold2023`

Complete calibrated digitization of all quantitative plots across PDF pages 1–20 of `corpus/actin_bundling/2023_Arnold.pdf` (Arnold, Gubbala & Takatori, *Phys. Rev. Lett.* 131, 128402 (2023); main article PDF pp. 1–6 / printed `128402-1` to `128402-6`; Supplemental Material PDF pp. 7–20 / printed `SI-1` to `SI-14`). All **28 calibrated figure readings** in [`figures.yaml`](figures.yaml) use `mode: code` Python readers under [`figures/`](figures/) that detect plot frames, axis ticks, markers, and curves directly from rendered PDF crops (600–1500 dpi), were visually verified on every magenta overlay PNG in `figwork/`, are wired into [`data.yaml`](data.yaml) (preserving all 95 prior text datums verbatim), are persisted in [`readings.json`](readings.json) (28/28 matching recipes), and are exercised by model runs and comparisons in [`runs.yaml`](runs.yaml) and [`comparisons.yaml`](comparisons.yaml).
