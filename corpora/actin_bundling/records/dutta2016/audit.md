## Verification Summary

- **Numbers & citations (`data.yaml`, `source.yaml`, `references.yaml`, PDF pp. 2–7):** Verified every scalar datum (`rho_star_1`, `Gamma_R_fig2_3`, `lambda_R_fig2_3a`, `lambda_A_fig2_3`, `Gamma_A_fig2_no_bundle`, `Gamma_A_fig2_finite`, `Gamma_A_fig2_macro`, `Gamma_A_fig3b`, `Gamma_R_fig4`, `lambda_A_fig4`, `screening_length_actin`, `filament_thickness_actin`), all 11 digitized curve metadata/conditions, bibliographic metadata, `printed_page` mappings, and all 45 references against `pages/page-002.png`–`page-007.png`. **Zero transcription errors found.**
- **Equations & Fourier analysis (`models/`, PDF pp. 3–6):** Independently re-derived the single-chain Debye factor $b(k^*) = (-8 + 8 e^{-k^{*2}/2} + 4k^{*2})/k^{*4}$ from the Gaussian directed-polymer propagator, the unnormalized 2D Fourier transform $\beta V(k) = 2\pi\Gamma_R/(k^2 + \lambda_R^{-2}) - \pi\Gamma_A\lambda_A^2 e^{-k^2\lambda_A^2/4}$ (verified numerically against radial Hankel quadrature to $10^{-14}$), the Hubbard-Stratonovich quadratic spinodal criterion $F(k^*) = \rho^* |\beta V(k^*)| b(k^*) > 1$, the analytical $k=0$ macrophase boundary, and all Lifshitz/asymptote/onset numbers reported in `q_2pi_fourier_mismatch`, `q_fig2_lambda_A_tweak`, `q_bundle_size_k0_choice`, and `q_actin_ratio_in_infinite_phase`. Confirmed that the stated models match the equations as printed, alternative conventions are properly placed in `variant_of`, and every model's `symbolic()` agrees with `run()` to $\le 10^{-14}$. **Zero equation/factor transcription errors found.**
- **Printed model results (`runs.yaml`, `comparisons.yaml`, PDF pp. 2–6):** All quantitative model predictions in the paper appear exclusively in Figs. 2–4 (11 curves total); all 11 are already digitized, run, and compared under both the stated angular-wavenumber models and the mixed-$2\pi$ `variant_of` models (except `macrophase_boundary_k0`, which is convention-independent at $k=0$). The qualitative text claim $F(\mathbf{k}) \ge 1$ at $\mathbf{k}=0$ for $\Gamma_A = 31$ (p. 4) is an inequality without rounding precision and its discrepancy ($F(0) = 0.911 < 1$) is documented in `q_fig2_lambda_A_tweak`; the qualitative actin claim ("a few filaments", p. 5) is documented in `q_actin_ratio_in_infinite_phase`. **No missing numerical comparisons.**
- **Unstated inputs (`added.yaml`):** All 9 added inputs have explicit physical bases, `stands_for` annotations, and realistic alternatives; none perturb exact mathematical identities.

## Changes and Disagreements Logged

- **`q_eq6_missing_prefactor_and_eq3_factor2` (added to `questions.yaml`, kind `possible_error`; PDF p. 3 / `28001-p2`, Eqs. 1, 3, 5, 6, 7):** Added a `possible_error` question documenting three additional derivation inconsistencies not recorded in `q_eq3_eq5_typos`: (1) Eq. (6) omits the $1/L$ factor (and consistent $\rho$ normalization) from the interaction coupling term inside $\int_0^L \mathrm{d}z$, making the $\mathrm{d}z$ integral dimensionally incompatible with $-\frac{\beta\epsilon}{2}\int_0^L \mathrm{d}z\,\mathbf{u}^2(z)$ if $\Omega$ is the local areal density as stated below Eq. - **`q_audit_solver_hardcoded_offset` (added to `questions.yaml`, kind `reading`; PDF p. 5 / `28001-p4`, Fig. 4):** Logged disagreement/solver audit note without modifying the first reader's `model.py`: `finite_bundle_boundary_eq8` (line 59) and `finite_bundle_boundary_eq8_mixed2pi` (line 61) hardcode `lo_lR = lR_inf + 1e-5` outside `settings`, causing false `None` returns near the Lifshitz point ($\Gamma_A \in (55.168, \sim 55.55)$ in mixed-$2\pi$, e.g., at $\Gamma_A = 55.5$ where $\lambda_{R,\text{fin}} - \lambda_{R,\inf} = 8.86\times 10^{-6} < 10^{-5}$; unaffected at digitized points $\Gamma_A \ge 64$); also noted unrefined `lR_max` (which truncates solutions near the vertical asymptote, e.g. $\Gamma_A = 83.55$), partial setting coverage in `refinements.yaml`, and the $3.3\times 10^{-8}$ cancellation jump at the hardcoded `k < 1e-2` Taylor switchover in `debye_factor_b` and downstream models.
