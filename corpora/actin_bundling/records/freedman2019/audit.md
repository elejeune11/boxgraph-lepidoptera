## New Open Questions and Reading Disagreements

- `q_fig2b_omits_initial_gap_energy` (p. 3 / 16194, Fig. 2B; `possible_error`): Fig. 2B prints Boltzmann weights `exp((mu_S - U(2 l_b))/k_B T)` and `exp((mu_L - U(4 l_b))/k_B T)` for filling an empty site inside a `6 l_b` gap, omitting the initial-gap energy term `+U(6 l_b)/k_B T` required in `exp(-Delta U_lat / k_B T)` by Eq. 2 (`U(6 l_b)/k_B T ~= 16.72` at `Delta l_xl = 100 nm`, `L_p = 17 um`, `l_b = 37 nm`). `equilibrium_lattice_mc_eq2` correctly implements full Eq. 2 `Delta U_lat`.
- `q_dissociation_constant_units_and_terminology` (p. 1 / 16192 & p. 5 / 16196; `reading`): disagreement with the first reader's choice to type `dissociation_constant_kd` (`K_d = k_off / k_on`) as dimensionless with `k_on` in `1/s` (AFINES MC rate convention) rather than molar `M` with second-order `k_on` in `M^-1 s^-1` as implied by p. 1 Ref. 8; preserved first reader's model per audit rules. Also notes Fig. 5E caption's loose phrasing calling `k_short^{off,1}` (`s^-1`) a "dissociation constant".
- `q_si_minor_typos_and_terminology` (p. 8 / SI-2, p. 9 / SI-3, p. 14 / SI-8, p. 15 / SI-9; `possible_error`): logs five additional minor SI text/caption slips not previously recorded: missing minus sign in "gradient of the potential energy `U_f(t)`" above SI Eq. 1 (p. 8); "unbinding if `j - 1` heads are already bound" below SI Eq. 4 meaning `j - 1` *other* heads (p. 8); SI Section B citing `Fig. 1C` instead of `Fig. 1D` for extracted domains (p. 9); missing unit `s` in Fig. S5 caption `"consistent 100 time window"` (p. 14); and Movie S1 caption listing `rho_short/rho_long = {0.5, 1, 2}` for `Fig. 1C-D` whereas Fig. 1C shows `{0.67, 1, 2}` (p. 15).
- `q_si_eq2_barbed_end_split_remainder` (p. 8 / SI-2, SI Section A below Eq. 2; `reading`): notes that resetting both split barbed-end links to rest length `l_a` strictly when `l_{a,1}(t) + Delta l_a > 2 l_a` discards overshoot above `2 l_a`, which would reduce realized contour growth rate below `k_g * Delta l_a` (to `(10/11) k_g Delta l_a` at `Delta l_a = 0.1 um`, `l_a = 1 um`) unless the code splits at `>= 2 l_a` or retains the remainder.
- `q_kmc_growth_protocol_and_cutoff_reading` (p. 3–4 / 16194–16195 & p. 12 / SI-6, Fig. 3A / Fig. S3A; `reading`): confirms that `kmc_growing_bundle_fig3` accurately implements Poisson growth + barbed-end binding (`N_diff/N_same ~= 0.0222`, `eps ~= 3.81`), and notes that allowing interior empty sites behind the leading cross-linker to fill still gives barbed-end `N_diff/N_same ~= 0.023`, whereas deterministic periodic site addition gives `~0.0142` (`eps ~= 4.26`), bracketing Fig. S3A's printed `0.016` / `4.1`; also records the minor `<1%` boundary choice of halting immediately at `cur_len == N_max`.
- `q_quantity_typing_reading` (p. 8 / SI-2 & p. 9 / SI-3; `reading`): records two minor quantity-typing disagreements preserved from the first reading: `afines_seed_crosslinkers_count` (`9` cross-linkers on first AFINES link) typed as `occupied_site_count` (lattice site count), and `force_lever_rule_si_eq5` typing instantaneous bead spacing `|r_{i+1} - r_i|` as rest link length `filament_link_length`.
