# Independent Audit of `haviv2008`

Source: Haviv, Gov, Ideses, Bernheim-Groswasser, *Eur. Biophys. J.* 37, 447–454 (2008), DOI `10.1007/s00249-007-0236-1`, PDF pages 1–10 (`pages/page-001.png`–`pages/page-010.png` and `text/page-001.txt`–`text/page-010.txt`, including Supporting Information pp. 9–10).

## Verification summary

- **Data (`data.yaml`, 20 entries):** Every printed number, sign, exponent, uncertainty, trailing zero, unit, page number, `printed_page`, `from_source`, and verbatim quote was verified against the page images for pp. 1–10 (including all 5 rows × 4 columns of Table 1 on p. 6 / 452, all rows of Supporting Table S1 on p. 10 / SI-2, and all body-text parameters and thresholds on pp. 2, 6, 7). Zero numerical transcription errors were found in `data.yaml`.
- **Models (`models/`, 18 models):** Every equation, symbol, sign, factor, `model.py` implementation, and `symbolic()` definition was checked against the page images on pp. 2, 4, 5, 6, 7. Exact Gaussian/`erfc` analytical moments in `eq1_distribution_moments_table/model.py` were independently verified against numerical quadrature (`scipy.integrate.quad`) across `N_min in {0, 1, 2}` and discrete summation over `N >= 1`.
- **Runs, comparisons, refinements, relations, added inputs, references:** All 19 runs, 14 comparisons, 1 refinement (`ref_eq1_max_xatol`), 5 model relations (all verified by `boxgraph relations`), 3 added inputs (`D_b_single_filament_added`, `N_min_moments_added`, `N_b_eval_phi3_added`, each with real alternatives and no verdict flips), and all 36 references on p. 8 / 454 (with corpus cross-links `borukhov2005`, `grason2007`, `henle2005`) were audited against the PDF and `papers.yaml`. No missing printed model results or missing corpus reference links were found.

- `on_rate_constant_scaling` (p. 7 / 453): Updated `equation` in `models/on_rate_constant_scaling/model.yaml` to state the printed `k_on propto n_a` explicitly alongside the constant-`D` `2 * k_on / D` port form (`model.py` unchanged).
- `on_rate_adsorption_scaling` (p. 7 / 453): Updated `equation` in `models/on_rate_adsorption_scaling/model.yaml` to state the printed `k_on propto n_af` explicitly alongside the constant-`D` `2 * k_on / D` port form (`model.py` unchanged).
- `on_rate_coat_hindered_scaling` (p. 7 / 453): Updated `equation` in `models/on_rate_coat_hindered_scaling/model.yaml` to state the printed `k_on propto n_af * k_off` explicitly alongside the constant-`D` `2 * k_on / D` port form (`model.py` unchanged).
- `cmp_on_rate_constant_vs_table1` (pp. 6–7 / 452–453): Corrected the note in `comparisons.yaml` from `+95% error at phi = 6/1` to `+100.9% error at phi = 6/1` (actual BoxGraph fitted constant `0.43374` vs printed `0.2159`).
- `cmp_naive_Db_mean_vs_table_s1_Nb_mean` (p. 10 / SI-2): Corrected the rounded value in the `comparisons.yaml` note for `phi = 6/1` from `70.8` to `70.7` (`(75.7 / 9)^2 = 70.7468`).
- `cmp_eq1_mean_Db_vs_table_s1` (pp. 6, 10 / 452, SI-2): Clarified the `comparisons.yaml` note that `phi = 1/5` (`-1.86 SE`) and `phi = 3/1` (`-1.63 SE`) are `close` at nominal `D_f = 9 nm` and graded `reproduced` by BoxGraph once the integer-digit rounding interval of `D_f = "9"` (`[8.5, 9.5] nm`) is propagated.
- `q_npeak_error_propagation_unstated` (p. 7 / 453, p. 4 / 450): Added an `unstated_input` open question to `questions.yaml` noting that the paper gives only `+/-20%` binning error on the on/off rates without stating the error-propagation formula or covariance for `N_peak = (k_on/k_off)^2`, whereas independent first-order propagation yields `+/-56.6%` (`+125%/-55.6%` worst-case) and a single-factor `+/-20%` gives `+/-40%` linear or `+44%/-36%` asymmetric vs Fig. 2d's roughly symmetric `+/-44%` bars.
- `q_fig4_and_threshold_wording_inconsistencies` (pp. 3, 5, 6 / 449, 451, 452): Added a `possible_error` open question to `questions.yaml` documenting three minor printed caption/text inconsistencies: Fig. 4 caption printing `[A]/[F] = 1/1, (A] = 5 uM` and `a–c Preformed ...` (erroneously spanning panel `b`, meant `a, c`); p. 451 col 1 attaching `(Fig. 4a, c)` after the monomeric-form clause; and p. 449 stating compact bundles form and were analyzed `above [F]/[A] = 1/5` despite p. 448 (`At [F]/[A] = 1/5 and above`) and Tables 1/S1 including `1/5`.

## Disagreements with first-reader judgements (logged, not altered)

- `mean_diameter_to_naive_filament_number` (p. 2 / 448, p. 10 / SI-2; logged in `q_audit_reading_reconstructed_and_relation_provenance`): Entered as a stated processing model citing p. 448 col 2 rather than marked `reconstructed:` (unlike `eq1_distribution_moments_table`), even though the paper computes `N_b = (D_b/D_f)^2` per bundle and never asserts `N_{b,mean} = (D_{b,mean}/D_f)^2`. Preserved as a first-reader choice; mitigated because `cmp_naive_Db_mean_vs_table_s1_Nb_mean` uses `purpose: approximation`.
- `rel_peak_adsorption_reduces_to_coat_hindered_zero_alpha` (p. 7 / 453; logged in `q_audit_reading_reconstructed_and_relation_provenance`): Tagged `provenance: stated` with `limit: {alpha: "0"}`. Although algebraically valid and verified by `boxgraph relations`, the paper derives `N_peak propto n_af^2` at nonzero `alpha ~= 0.032` via cancellation of `k_off` in `(n_af * k_off / k_off)^2`, not by taking an `alpha -> 0` limit of the adsorption-only peak model. Preserved as a first-reader judgement.
