## Disagreements & minor editorial observations (no deletion or override of first-reader judgements)

- `k_off_fascin_domains` (p. 21 / SI-9): The `data.yaml` note transcribes the Fig. S5D inset as `k_off = 0.198 s^-1`, whereas `page-021.png` actually prints `0.198 s^1` (missing minus sign). The recorded value `0.198` and unit `1/s` are physically correct; the missing minus sign is logged in `q_figs5de_offset_concentrations_and_unit_typo` rather than altering the unit.
- `d_interfil_fascin_lit`, `d_interfil_actinin_lit` (p. 6 / 2701, p. 7 / 2702): Fig. 4A and 4C schematics (p. 6 PNG, citing `[8, 14]`) label predicted spacings as `10 nm` and `34 nm`, and Discussion (p. 7) states `~10 nm` for compact bundlers, slightly different from `~8 nm` and `~35 nm` in p. 6 text and Fig. 6A (p. 9). Left out of `data.yaml` per first-reader scope.
- `source.yaml` / SI p. 25 (SI-13): Minor non-quantitative typo noted in SI Plasmid construction (`"6XHis-SNAP-espins 2A and 3B"`, whereas Protein purification and Fig. 5 exclusively use espin isoform `2B` / `E2B`), and p. 7 right column cites `[23–25]` for fimbrin/α-actinin actin conformation whereas p. 7 left column cites `[25–27]`.
