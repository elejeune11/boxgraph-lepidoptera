## Disagreements with first-reader choices (logged as `reading` questions)

- `q_audit_filament_radius_vs_spacing_ambiguity` (pp. 1–2, `011916-1`–`011916-2`): Logged `reading` question noting that (1) p. 2 col 1 prose calls `a ~ 10 nm` the filament+cross-linker "radius" (consistent with `N_b = (R/a)^2`), whereas Fig. 1(b) draws `a` as the center-to-center lattice spacing (effective diameter) and co-cited Ref. [6] (`haviv2008`, co-authored by Gov) uses `9 nm` as the effective diameter; and (2) `filament_radius_a_printed` sets `from_source: ref-5` (not in corpus) while the paper co-cites `[5,6]` including corpus record `ref-6` (`haviv2008`).
- `q_audit_incompressible_gaussian_tail_unprinted_prefactor` (p. 4, `011916-4`): Logged `reading` question disagreeing with the asymmetric treatment of unprinted dimensional prefactors across the p. 4 col 2 tail models: `empty_shell_tail_printed` is marked `runnable: false` because the prefactor inside `exp(...)` is omitted, whereas `incompressible_gaussian_tail` supplies the unprinted derived coefficient `2*beta*K*rho*a^2/(D_R*L_p^4)` inside `exp[...]` (for printed `P(R) ~ exp(-R^2)`) without marking `from_words` or `reconstructed`, and `surface_monopole_tail_printed/model.py` normalizes printed `R` to `R/a` and maps `k` to `k_s_prime`.
- `q_audit_empty_shell_added_alternatives_cross_threshold` (p. 3, `011916-3`): Logged `reading` question disagreeing with the one-at-a-time alternatives in `added.yaml` relative to baseline `E_c = 25 pN = 2*E_c*`: three alternatives (`eps_b_added = 4.0 pN`, `L_defect_spacing_added = 100 nm`, `lam_shell_added = 10 nm`) hit or exceed the threshold `E_c <= E_c*`, causing `run_equilibrium_radius_shell_eq3` and `run_equilibrium_radius_shell_from_energy` to silently return `R_0 = None` instead of probing finite-width sensitivity.

## New open questions added (non-`reading`)

- `q_audit_low_conc_scaling_shell_and_bulk` (`possible_error`, p. 4, `011916-4`): Documents why `cmp_low_conc_exp_shell` and `cmp_low_conc_exp_bulk_monopole` are `not reproduced`: the weak-binding scaling errors in Eqs. (3) and (7) propagate through the Langmuir binding relation, leaving only `R_{0,s} ~ c` and `R'_{0,b} ~ c^(1/3)` valid at low `c` and `eps`.
- `q_audit_eq10_flux_dimensions` (`unstated_input`, p. 4, `011916-4`): Documents the dimensional and thermodynamic-force ambiguities in Eq. (10) (`N_dot = (2*R/a^2)*R_dot = 2*pi*R*a*rho_ac*(1/gamma)*(-dE_total/dR)`), where the prose describes `N_dot` as flux "per unit length" although `N = (R/a)^2` is dimensionless (`N_dot` in `1/[time]`) and `2*pi*R*a` introduces an unexplained axial length `a`.
