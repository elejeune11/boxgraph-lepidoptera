## Changes and Open Questions Added

- **`q_eq8_missing_length_ratio_and_large_alpha_figS11`** (`questions.yaml`, PDF pp. 11–12 & 23 / SI-5–SI-6 & SI-17): Added `possible_error` open question noting that relating elongated tactoid volume `V(alpha) ~ (K/gamma)^3 * alpha^5` to colloid volume `V_c ~ a^3` introduces an unstated dimensionless ratio `beta = K / (gamma * a)` (`beta ~= 5.5` experimentally, `beta^(-6/5) ~= 0.13`) that Fig. S11 implicitly sets to `beta = 1` (`alpha_1^5 + alpha_2^5 = V_d/V_c`), and that evaluating Eq. 8 across Fig. S11 (`V_d/V_c` in `[1, 40]`) pushes tip angles to `alpha* in [0.87, 1.82] rad` (`> pi/2` for `V_d/V_c > 19.1`) and `alpha_1 = 1 rad` (`alpha_2` up to `2.08 rad`) on the red boundary, outside the small-angle approximation.
- **`q_F0_overloaded_definitions_main_vs_si`** (`questions.yaml`, PDF pp. 4, 9–11 / PNAS p. 11128 & SI-3–SI-5): Added `unexplained_difference` open question documenting that the paper uses the single symbol `F_0` for three inconsistent definitions: Onsager full-rod energy `F_0 ~ D_M L_M L_a c_a k_B T` (proportional to `L_M`, independent of `y_c`, SI p. 9), Eq. 4 prefactor `F_0 = f_0 * y_c` (proportional to `y_c`, independent of `L_M`, SI p. 10), and Main p. 4 harmonic energy scale `F_0 = k * L_D^2 / 2` (larger than Eq. 5's `F_0` by `2x` for `L_D >> L_M` and `2.36x` at `L_D = 3.2 um, L_M = 1.5 um`, while Eq. 4 diverges at the poles).
- **`q_fig2c_fit_type_axis_and_unstated_params`** (`questions.yaml`, PDF pp. 3 & 9 / PNAS p. 11127 & SI-3): Added `possible_error` open question noting that Main p. 3 col 1 refers to Fig. 2C's y-axis as `d/L_D` whereas the figure plots `d / 2L_D`, Main p. 3 col 2 cites Fig. 2C Inset for a linear fit to the early trajectory (50 s on SI p. 9) whereas the Fig. 2C caption calls the curve an exponential fit (plotted over 0–1000 s on an inverted y-axis), and the exponential fit parameters/timescale are never stated.
- **`q_fig2b_central_40_vs_60_percent`** (`questions.yaml`, PDF pp. 3 & 8 / PNAS p. 11127 & SI-2): Added `possible_error` open question noting that SI p. 8 splits each tactoid into 5 regions of width `0.2` in `d_i / L_D` plotted at left bin edges `0, 0.2, 0.4, 0.6, 0.8` in Fig. 2B, so the `~90%` (`~89%`) sum over the first three plotted points (`0, 0.2, 0.4`) spans `[0, 0.6]` (the central 60% of the droplet long axis) rather than the `"central 40%"` stated on Main p. 3 (the first two bins `[0, 0.4]` sum to only `~63%`).
